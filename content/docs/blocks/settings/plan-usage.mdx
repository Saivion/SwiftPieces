Where a subscriber checks their plan. A summary card names the plan with a tinted status chip, its price and when it renews, ends or leaves its trial; usage meters fill to what has been used and turn to warning and danger near a limit. Upgrade works, confirms and folds away on the top plan, and Manage subscription and Restore purchases use StoreKit by default.
