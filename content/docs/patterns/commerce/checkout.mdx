The checkout section of a cart or checkout screen. Each bag line has a quantity stepper, and the line price, summary and Pay label roll as quantities change; delivery shows the address with Change and each speed with its day and price, and a promo code applies or explains why not. Pay shows its working, done and failed states in place, Apple Pay uses the system button, and the block settles into the placed order with its number and arrival day.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Checkout in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/checkout).
