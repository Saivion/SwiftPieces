The list of past conversations. A search field filters by title and last line as you type, and chats sit under Pinned, Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days and Earlier. Swipe a row for Pin and Delete or hold it for Rename, Pin, Share and Delete; rename edits the title in place, and delete confirms, fades the row while your closure runs and restores it with the error if it fails.
