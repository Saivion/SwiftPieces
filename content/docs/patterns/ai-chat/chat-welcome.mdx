The empty start of a new chat. A greeting with the person's name sits over a prompt line, intents (Write, Learn, Plan, Code) pick a two-row grid of suggestion cards that pages sideways, and a tapped card hands its prompt to your app and marks itself. Shuffle fetches fresh suggestions with its own working and failed states, and Continue where you left off lists the three most recent chats with See all.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Chat Welcome in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/chat-welcome).
