A conversation where the person shares photos and files. Their photos lay out one large, two side by side or in a grid with +N, files show as chips with a type icon, name and size, and each attachment shows its upload progress or Retry. The assistant's replies can return files as cards that open in Quick Look or share, and tapping a photo opens a full-screen viewer with zoom.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Attachment Chat in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/chat-attachments).
