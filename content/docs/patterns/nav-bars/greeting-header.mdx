A home tab's header in place of a plain large title. Today's date, the notifications bell with an unread badge and the avatar sit on one row over a greeting for the time of day and a search field. As content scrolls the greeting and search fold away into a compact bar, driven by the scroll offset on iOS 18 or by any value you pass.
