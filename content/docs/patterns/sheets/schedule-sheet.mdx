The sheet for send later, a reminder or a booking. Quick picks resolve to real times and hide once they pass, Pick date and time opens a graphical calendar and a time picker in place, and Repeat covers Never, Daily, Weekdays, Weekly and custom days. Schedule names the chosen moment, runs your closure with a Schedule value and shows working, Scheduled and failed.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Schedule Sheet in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/schedule-sheet).
