A primer shown before a system permission prompt. It explains why the app needs the permission, lists what it makes possible, draws the alert iOS will show next, then requests the real permission. Granted turns to a check and calls back; denied explains where to change it and offers Open Settings, and turns granted when the person comes back having allowed it.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Permission Primer in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/permission-primer).
