The content of a share and actions sheet for a post, article or file. A header shows the item, recent people and conversations sit in a strip you select from before sending with an optional message, and action tiles copy the link, save it, add it to a collection or open the system share sheet. Report confirms first, and every action shows its working, done and failed states.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Share Sheet in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/share-sheet).
