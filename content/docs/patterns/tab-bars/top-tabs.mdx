A strip of tabs over a pager of your pages. The accent indicator follows the pager continuously, stretching to each tab's width, tapping a tab pages to it, and the strip scrolls to keep the selected tab in the middle. Tabs can show counts, and three or fewer share the width while more scroll.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Top Tabs in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/top-tabs).
