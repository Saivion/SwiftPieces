Four tabs in one floating capsule, the selected one on a quiet pill, with a round accent create button beside it; each tab is a symbol over its label with an optional badge. The create button opens a menu of quick actions above the bar over a scrim, runs the one you pick and shows working, done and failed itself. Tapping the selected tab again calls your closure to scroll it to the top.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Action Tab Bar in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/action-tab-bar).
