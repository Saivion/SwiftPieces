The profile step right after sign up, with where the person is in your first run, a large title and minimal fields. The avatar takes a photo from the system picker or shows initials on one of five theme colours, the username is cleaned as it's typed and checked for availability through your closure, and an optional role and birthday follow. Continue runs your closure through working, done and failed, and Skip for now leaves without saving.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Profile Setup in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/profile-setup).
