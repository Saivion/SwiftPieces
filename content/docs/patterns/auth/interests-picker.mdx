Topics as chips with a symbol and a label, grouped under section titles in a flow layout that fits any width and text size. Picked chips take a light accent tint with a check, the count rolls, a search filters as it's typed, and the button counts down Pick 2 more until the minimum turns it into Continue, which runs your closure through working, done and failed. An optional maximum steps the other chips back.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Interests Picker in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/interests-picker).
