An email field that checks itself and Continue with email, which runs your closure through working, done and failed. The card then turns to Check your inbox with the address, Open Mail and Resend on a countdown, plus an optional six-digit code that fills from AutoFill and submits itself. Links that open your app sign the person in through `onLink`.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Email Link Sign In in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/magic-link).
