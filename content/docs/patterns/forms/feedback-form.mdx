The send-feedback section of a Help or Settings screen. A numbered rating, Bug, Idea, Billing and Other chips that change the message's prompt, up to three screenshots from the system picker, and Include device info that lists exactly what it sends. Send checks for a topic and a message, shows its working and failed states, and a thank-you replaces the form once it lands.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Feedback Form in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/feedback-form).
