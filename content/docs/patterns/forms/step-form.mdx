A container for forms that take several steps, such as a booking or a new project. Each step is your own view with a validity flag, so Continue waits until it's complete, and steps slide in from the side they come from. Review sums up the answers each step reports with Edit links that jump back, and Submit shows working, done and failed.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Step Form in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/step-form).
