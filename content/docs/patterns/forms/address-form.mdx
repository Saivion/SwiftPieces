The address section of a checkout, account or delivery screen. Every line carries its AutoFill type, the country opens a searchable list and relabels the region and postal code for it, and street suggestions from MapKit fill the city, region and postal code when picked. Fields check on Save and then live, and Save shows working, done and failed.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Address Form in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/address-form).
