The edit-profile section of an account screen or sheet. The photo changes through the system picker, the username checks availability through your closure and offers suggestions when it's taken, and the bio counts toward its limit. Save wakes only when something changed and everything is valid, shows working, done and failed, and `hasChanges` lets your sheet confirm before discarding edits.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Profile Form in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/profile-form).
