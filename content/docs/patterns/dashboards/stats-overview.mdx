The section at the top of an analytics or dashboard screen. One card holds the headline metric with its change against the previous period and a Swift Charts line you drag across to read any day, with the previous period dashed beneath and a 1W to 1Y control that morphs the line. Metric tiles sit below, and loading, empty and failed states are built in.
