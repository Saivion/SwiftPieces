The part of Settings every app with accounts needs. A profile card changes the photo and the name in place, appearance shows System, Light and Dark as small screens in your theme's colours, and notification toggles know when iOS has notifications off. Export my data, Sign out and Delete account each confirm and show their own working, done and failed states.
