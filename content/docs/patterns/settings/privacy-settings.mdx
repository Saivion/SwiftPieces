Where people see what the app may use and what it does with their data. Each permission you declare shows its real status from iOS, asks there and then when it was never asked, and opens Settings otherwise. Data toggles, Download my data with working, done and failed states, blocked accounts and the policies follow.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Privacy Settings in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/privacy-settings).
