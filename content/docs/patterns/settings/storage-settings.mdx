Where people see and reclaim the room an app takes. A summary card shows the total, the device's free space and a bar split into downloads, cache, photos and other; Clear cache confirms, works and shrinks its segment as the figures count down. Downloads remove from Edit or a long press, and the options cover cellular, quality and when finished downloads go.
