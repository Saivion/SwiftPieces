Where a person sees what an agent intends to do and follows it as it runs. In review the steps are numbered and can be renamed, removed, reordered or added to before Approve plan; once running, each step reads pending, running with its notes, done, failed with Retry step, or skipped, and the card folds to a one-line summary.
