The notifications list of a workspace, social or commerce app. Rows group into New, Earlier this week and Older, each with the actor's photo or initials, one sentence with the names in semibold, the time and an unread dot, and invites, mentions and shares carry inline buttons that show working, done and failed in place. Swipe or long-press to mark read, unread or delete, and Mark all as read clears every dot with a rolling count.
