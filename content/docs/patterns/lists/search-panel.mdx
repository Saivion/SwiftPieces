Drop it under a `.searchable` field. Before typing it shows recent searches with remove and Clear all and suggested searches as chips; while typing it asks your closure for debounced suggestions with the typed text highlighted; after a search it shows scope chips with counts and a card of results per scope, with loading, failed and no-results states built in.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Search Panel in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/search-panel).
