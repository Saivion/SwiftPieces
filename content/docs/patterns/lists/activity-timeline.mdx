For order history, account activity or a project log. Events group under Today, Yesterday and dated headers, each with a node on a hairline rail that is tinted only for a real outcome, a title and time, a detail line and an optional attachment chip. Runs of one kind fold into one line that opens in place, a menu filters by kind, and Show earlier activity pages back with its working, failed and end states.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Activity Timeline in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/activity-timeline).
