A composer pinned above the keyboard that opens a suggestion panel when a word starts with / or @. Commands filter as you type, mentions search through your closure with Searching, no matches and Retry, and a pick becomes a chip above the text that onSend receives as a structured token.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Command Composer in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/composer-mentions).
