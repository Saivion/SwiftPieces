A composer pinned above the keyboard whose mic turns the bar into a recording state, with a level meter that moves with the voice, the elapsed time, Cancel and Done, while the transcript fills the field. It runs on the Speech framework and AVAudioEngine, asks for access and offers Settings when it is off, and takes your own transcription for previews or another engine.

Its SwiftUI source comes with SwiftPieces Pro: [Dictation Composer in the Pro library](https://pro.swiftpieces.com/library/patterns/composer-dictation).
