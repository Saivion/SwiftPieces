Overview and detail for a long series: a chart of the window you are reading, and a strip of the whole series under it with a glass lens you drag and resize to choose that window. The readout names the window's summary (the average by default) in a large numeral over its dates. The detail chart draws the window's samples as bars in the text color, with the window's average as a red dashed rule and an "avg" chip on the value axis; it pans with the lens and rescales without overshooting. The strip draws every sample in one `Canvas`, bright inside the window and dim outside, over the lens: a liquid glass slab with a glass handle joined to each end. Drag the slab to move the window, drag a handle (or within about 22 points of an edge) to resize it, tap the strip to centre the lens there, and double-tap to show everything and again to go back. Vertical swipes that start on the strip still scroll the screen or move a sheet. The bars are content; the lens is the control, so it is the only glass.

## Notes

- Pass the whole series as `[RangeLens.Sample]` (a date and a value each) and bind the window as a `ClosedRange<Date>`, from the date of its first sample to the date of its last. Keep both in your own state; `RangeLens.window(last: 42, of: days)` gives a good opening window, the last six weeks of daily samples. Samples are sorted by date for you; negative and non-finite values draw as zero.
- The readout names the window's `summary` (`.average`, the default, or `.total`, `.maximum`, `.minimum`) in a large numeral, with your `unit` beside it and the window's dates under it ("3 Mar – 14 Apr", with the year when the window crosses one or lies in an earlier year). `valueLabel` formats the numeral and what VoiceOver reads, for example a currency or minutes as a `Measurement`.
- The detail chart draws the window's samples as bars in the text color. Its ceiling follows the window's highest sample with a little headroom, so the bars always fill it; when the window changes, the ceiling moves on the value role and never overshoots, and the gridlines, on round values at most four, slide with it. The window's average is a red dashed rule with an "avg" chip on the value axis (`showsAverage: false` hides it); the rule follows the bars while they move.
- The strip draws every sample on one scale in one `Canvas`, on a quiet track: dim outside the window, bright inside it. The lens is the only glass: a slab over the window with a narrow glass handle joined to each end by a liquid neck, all in one `PieceLiquidGroup`. Bars outside the window lie under the glass; the window's bars sit on the slab, crisp. Month marks run under the strip (years for long series, weeks or days for short ones), thinned evenly so they never crowd, and January names its year.
- Drag the slab to move the window: it follows the finger, swells a little while held, and past either end of the series gives with rubber-band resistance. Drag a handle, or within about 22 points of an edge, to resize: the handle swells and floods red while held, stops firmly at `minimumSpan` samples (a week by default) and never inverts. Let go and the lens coasts a little with the flick and lands on whole samples. A drag that starts off the lens brings the lens to the finger first.
- Tap the strip beside the lens to centre the lens there. Double-tap to show the whole series, and double-tap again to go back to the window before it. A press held still for a moment also takes hold, for a careful move.
- The selection is written live while the lens moves, whenever the whole samples it rests on change, and once more when it lands. Set the selection yourself and the lens glides there; a window wider than the series shows all of it.
- Horizontal drags and held presses belong to the lens; vertical swipes that start on the strip still scroll the screen or move a sheet, since the strip's recognizers only begin for a mostly horizontal drag.
- Haptics: a selection tick when the moving edge crosses into another week (another month for weekly samples, a year for monthly ones), a light tap when a handle is grabbed and on Show all, and a soft bump when the lens meets an end of the series or its minimum span. Never per frame.
- States: an empty series shows `messages.empty` in place of the numeral, a baseline and the bare track, with no lens; one sample shows that sample; all zeros draw a flat strip and a zero axis. While your data loads, apply `.redacted(reason: .placeholder)`: the bars draw as quiet placeholders and the text redacts. Hundreds of samples stay smooth: both charts are single `Canvas` passes and the series is sorted and summed once per change, never on a drag step.
- VoiceOver reads the readout ("Average 8,412 steps a day, 3 March to 14 April"), then the detail chart, which also plays the window's samples as an audio graph, then the lens: an adjustable element named "Range" whose value is the dates and the summary. Swipe up or down to move it a step (a week of daily samples); the Widen, Narrow and Show all (then Back to previous range) actions change its size and announce the result.
- Hardware keyboard: with the strip focused, the left and right arrows move the window a step, up and down widen and narrow it, and Space toggles Show all.
- Reduce Motion: the lens lands and resizes on a short spring with no overshoot and doesn't swell, the detail cross-fades to a new scale instead of growing, and the numeral and dates cross-fade instead of rolling. Under the finger the numeral and dates change in place in every mode, so they stay legible.
- Dynamic Type scales the readout, the axis values and the marks (the axis and marks stop growing before they crowd the charts); the numeral and unit stack when they no longer fit on one line. Right to left, time runs from the right: the latest samples, the lens and the value axis sit on the left. All text is one weight, semibold.
- `Style` sets the accent (the average rule, its chip and a held handle) and the ink on it, the text, secondary and axis colors, the dim color of the bars outside the window (a solid mix into the ground, never transparency), the gridlines, the track, the detail chart's height and the numeral size. Under Increase Contrast the bars outside the window take the secondary color. `Messages` replaces any line of built-in copy, such as `average: "Daily average"`.

    /// The last `count` samples of a series as a selection, a good place to open: `RangeLens.window(last: 42, of: days)`
    /// for the last six weeks of daily samples. An empty series gives today.
    public static func window(last count: Int, of samples: [Sample]) -> ClosedRange<Date> {
        let dates = samples.map(\.date).sorted()
        guard let last = dates.last else { return Date()...Date() }
        let first = dates[max(dates.count - max(count, 1), 0)]
        return first...last
    }

    public var body: some View {
        let model = cache.model(for: RangeLensModel.Key(samples: samples, calendar: calendar, locale: locale))
        let shown = hasSpan ? span : model.span(for: selection, minimum: minimumSpan)
        let window = model.window(of: shown, minimum: minimumSpan)
        let ceiling = RangeLensScale(peak: window.map { model.peak($0) } ?? 0)

        VStack(alignment: .leading, spacing: 18) {
            readout(model, window)
            detail(model, shown, window, scale: ceiling)
            strip(model, shown, window)
        }
        .frame(maxWidth: .infinity, alignment: .leading)
        .fontWeight(.semibold)
        .saturation(isEnabled ? 1 : 0)
        .onAppear {
            guard !hasSpan else { return }
            span = model.span(for: selection, minimum: minimumSpan)
            hasSpan = true
            top = ceiling.top
        }
        // A window set from outside: the lens glides there. Its own writes are recognised and left alone.
        .onChange(of: selection) { _, new in
            guard grab == nil, new != written else { return }
            let target = model.span(for: new, minimum: minimumSpan)
            guard target != span else { return }
            withAnimation(motion.morph) { span = target }
        }
        // New data: the lens keeps its dates.
        .onChange(of: model.revision) { _, _ in
            span = model.span(for: selection, minimum: minimumSpan)
            previous = nil
        }
        // The detail rescales on the value role, so the bars never grow past their true height. Under Reduce Motion
        // the chart cross-fades to the new scale instead (see `detail`).
        .onChange(of: ceiling.top) { _, new in
            if reduceMotion {
                withAnimation(.easeInOut(duration: 0.25)) { top = new }
            } else {
                withAnimation(motion.value) { top = new }
            }
        }
        .sensoryFeedback(.selection, trigger: tick)
        .sensoryFeedback(.impact(weight: .light), trigger: grabTick)
        .sensoryFeedback(.impact(flexibility: .soft, intensity: 0.7), trigger: limitTick)
    }

    private func readout(_ model: RangeLensModel, _ window: ClosedRange<Int>?) -> some View {
        VStack(alignment: .leading, spacing: 3) {
            Text(summaryTitle)
                .font(.system(size: captionSize, weight: .semibold))
                .foregroundStyle(style.secondary)
            if let window {
                let value = model.value(summary, in: window)
                ViewThatFits(in: .horizontal) {
                    HStack(alignment: .firstTextBaseline, spacing: 6) {
                        numeral(value)
                        unitLabel
                    }
                    VStack(alignment: .leading, spacing: 2) {
                        numeral(value)
                        unitLabel
                    }
                }
                let dates = model.dateRange(window, calendar: calendar, locale: locale)
                // Under the finger the dates change at once, so a fast drag never ghosts; a programmatic change rolls.
                Text(dates)
                    .font(.system(size: captionSize, weight: .semibold))
                    .monospacedDigit()
                    .foregroundStyle(style.secondary)
                    .contentTransition(grab != nil ? .identity : reduceMotion ? .opacity : .numericText())
                    .animation(grab == nil ? motion.value : nil, value: dates)
            } else {
                Text(messages.empty)
                    .font(.system(size: unitSize + 2, weight: .semibold))
                    .foregroundStyle(style.secondary)
                    .padding(.vertical, 6)
            }
        }
        .frame(maxWidth: .infinity, alignment: .leading)
        .accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)
        .accessibilityLabel(window.map { readoutSentence(model, $0) } ?? messages.empty)
    }

    private func numeral(_ value: Double) -> some View {
        Text(valueLabel(value))
            .font(.system(size: numeralSize, weight: .semibold))
            .monospacedDigit()
            .tracking(-0.4)
            .foregroundStyle(style.text)
            .lineLimit(1)
            .minimumScaleFactor(0.6)
            // Under a finger it changes in place, so it stays legible; a programmatic change rolls on the value role.
            .contentTransition(grab != nil ? .identity : reduceMotion ? .opacity : .numericText(value: value))
            .animation(grab == nil ? motion.value : nil, value: value)
    }

    @ViewBuilder private var unitLabel: some View {
        if let unit, !unit.isEmpty {
            Text(unit)
                .font(.system(size: unitSize, weight: .semibold))
                .foregroundStyle(style.secondary)
        }
    }

    // MARK: Detail chart

    private func detail(_ model: RangeLensModel, _ shown: RangeLensSpan, _ window: ClosedRange<Int>?, scale: RangeLensScale) -> some View {
        let input = RangeLensPainter.Detail(
            model: model,
            step: scale.step,
            showsAverage: showsAverage && window != nil && !redacted,
            redacted: redacted,
            rtl: isRTL,
            pad: chartPad,
            axisSize: axisSize,
            chipHeight: chipHeight,
            averageMark: messages.averageMark,
            displayScale: displayScale,
            text: style.text,
            label: style.tertiary,
            grid: style.grid,
            accent: style.accent,
            accentInk: style.accentInk
        )
        return RangeLensAnimated(value: top) { top in
            RangeLensAnimatedSpan(start: shown.start, end: shown.end) { start, end in
                Canvas { context, size in
                    RangeLensPainter.draw(input, start: start, end: end, top: top, in: &context, size: size)
                }
            }
        }
        // Under Reduce Motion a new scale is a new picture that cross-fades in, rather than bars that move.
        .id(reduceMotion ? top : 0)
        .transition(.opacity)
        .frame(height: style.chartHeight + chartPad * 2)
        .accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)
        .accessibilityLabel(messages.chart)
        .accessibilityValue(window.map { readoutSentence(model, $0) } ?? messages.empty)
        .accessibilityChartDescriptor(RangeLensChart(model: model, window: window, title: messages.chart, dateAxis: messages.dateAxis, valueAxis: unit ?? summaryTitle, rightToLeft: isRTL, valueLabel: valueLabel))
    }

    // MARK: Strip and lens

    private func strip(_ model: RangeLensModel, _ shown: RangeLensSpan, _ window: ClosedRange<Int>?) -> some View {
        GeometryReader { proxy in
            let geometry = RangeLensGeometry(width: proxy.size.width, count: model.count, handle: Self.handleWidth, minimumLens: 20)
            let input = RangeLensPainter.Strip(model: model, geometry: geometry, rtl: isRTL, rowHeight: Self.lensHeight, barInset: Self.barInset, axisSize: axisSize, redacted: redacted, inside: style.text, outside: contrast == .increased ? style.secondary : style.dim, label: style.tertiary, baseline: style.grid, track: style.track)
            ZStack(alignment: .topLeading) {
                // The bars outside the window lie under the glass, so a handle passes over them as a lens would; the
                // window's own bars sit on the slab, crisp.
                RangeLensAnimatedSpan(start: shown.start, end: shown.end) { start, end in
                    Canvas { context, size in
                        RangeLensPainter.drawStrip(input, layer: .under, start: start, end: end, in: &context, size: size)
                    }
                }
                if model.count > 0 {
                    lens(geometry, shown)
                }
                RangeLensAnimatedSpan(start: shown.start, end: shown.end) { start, end in
                    Canvas { context, size in
                        RangeLensPainter.drawStrip(input, layer: .over, start: start, end: end, in: &context, size: size)
                    }
                }
                .allowsHitTesting(false)
                RangeLensTouchSurface(isEnabled: isEnabled && model.count > 1) { event in
                    handle(event, geometry: geometry, model: model)
                }
            }
        }
        .frame(height: Self.lensHeight + axisRow)
        // Drawn and touched in physical coordinates; right to left is mirrored by hand, so time runs from the right.
        .environment(\.layoutDirection, .leftToRight)
        .accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)
        .accessibilityLabel(messages.range)
        .accessibilityValue(window.map { lensValue(model, $0) } ?? messages.empty)
        .accessibilityHint(model.count > 1 ? messages.hint : "")
        .accessibilityAdjustableAction { direction in
            move(by: direction == .increment ? model.step : -model.step, model: model, announce: false)
        }
        .accessibilityAction(named: messages.widen) { resize(by: model.step, model: model) }
        .accessibilityAction(named: messages.narrow) { resize(by: -model.step, model: model) }
        .accessibilityAction(named: isAll(model) && previous != nil ? messages.showPrevious : messages.showAll) { toggleAll(model: model, announce: true) }
        .accessibilityHidden(model.count == 0)
        .focusable(isEnabled && model.count > 1)
        .onKeyPress(.leftArrow, phases: [.down, .repeat]) { _ in
            move(by: isRTL ? model.step : -model.step, model: model, announce: false)
            return .handled
        }
        .onKeyPress(.rightArrow, phases: [.down, .repeat]) { _ in
            move(by: isRTL ? -model.step : model.step, model: model, announce: false)
            return .handled
        }
        .onKeyPress(.upArrow, phases: [.down, .repeat]) { _ in
            resize(by: model.step, model: model)
            return .handled
        }
        .onKeyPress(.downArrow, phases: [.down, .repeat]) { _ in
            resize(by: -model.step, model: model)
            return .handled
        }
        .onKeyPress(.space) {
            toggleAll(model: model, announce: false)
            return .handled
        }
    }

    /// The lens: a glass slab over the window with a glass handle joined to each end. Held, the slab swells a little;
    /// a held handle swells and floods red. The swell and the tint move on their own clock (press and release), so
    /// they never ride the spring the lens lands on.
    private func lens(_ geometry: RangeLensGeometry, _ shown: RangeLensSpan) -> some View {
        let frame = geometry.lens(shown)
        let left = isRTL ? geometry.width - frame.upperBound : frame.lowerBound
        let width = frame.upperBound - frame.lowerBound
        let held = grab?.part
        let swell: CGFloat = held == .body && !reduceMotion ? 3 : 0
        let leading: RangeLensPart = isRTL ? .upper : .lower
        let trailing: RangeLensPart = isRTL ? .lower : .upper
        return PieceLiquidGroup {
            HStack(spacing: PieceLiquid.joined) {
                grip(held == leading)
                Color.clear
                    .pieceLiquid(RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 12 + swell, style: .continuous), interactive: false)
                    .padding(-swell)
                    .frame(width: width, height: Self.lensHeight)
                grip(held == trailing)
            }
            .offset(x: left - Self.handleWidth - PieceLiquid.joined)
        }
        .frame(width: geometry.width, height: Self.lensHeight, alignment: .leading)
        .accessibilityHidden(true)
    }

    private func grip(_ held: Bool) -> some View {
        Capsule()
            .fill(held ? style.accentInk : style.secondary)
            .frame(width: 3, height: 16)
            .frame(width: Self.handleWidth, height: Self.lensHeight)
            .pieceLiquid(Capsule(), tint: held ? style.accent : nil, interactive: false)
            .pieceLiquidScale(held && !reduceMotion ? 1.12 : 1)
    }

    // MARK: Touch

    private func handle(_ event: RangeLensTouchEvent, geometry: RangeLensGeometry, model: RangeLensModel) {
        guard model.count > 1, geometry.slot > 0 else { return }
        let n = Double(model.count)
        switch event {
        case let .began(point, _):
            let x = geometry.time(point.x, rtl: isRTL)
            var origin = span
            var part = geometry.part(at: x, of: span)
            if part == nil {
                // Off the lens: it comes to the finger, centred there, and the drag carries on from there.
                let width = span.end - span.start
                origin = clamp(RangeLensSpan(start: (geometry.slot(at: x) - width / 2).rounded(), end: 0), width: width, n: n)
                withAnimation(motion.snap) { span = origin }
                blendUntil = .now + 0.24
                part = .body
                tick += 1
            }
            let held = part ?? .body
            withAnimation(motion.press) { grab = RangeLensGrab(part: held, origin: origin, x: x) }
            lastBucket = model.bucket(edge(of: origin, part: held, n: model.count))
            atLimit = false
            if held != .body { grabTick += 1 }
        case let .moved(point):
            guard let grab else { return }
            let x = geometry.time(point.x, rtl: isRTL)
            let next = tracked(grab, x: x, geometry: geometry, n: n)
            if Date.now < blendUntil {
                withAnimation(motion.press) { span = next.span }
            } else {
                span = next.span
            }
            if next.limited != atLimit {
                atLimit = next.limited
                if next.limited { limitTick += 1 }
            }
            let bucket = model.bucket(edge(of: next.span, part: grab.part, n: model.count))
            if bucket != lastBucket {
                lastBucket = bucket
                tick += 1
            }
            write(next.span, model: model)
        case let .ended(_, velocity):
            release(velocity: isRTL ? -velocity : velocity, geometry: geometry, model: model)
        case .cancelled:
            release(velocity: 0, geometry: geometry, model: model)
        case let .tapped(point):
            let x = geometry.time(point.x, rtl: isRTL)
            guard geometry.part(at: x, of: span) == nil else { return }
            let width = span.end - span.start
            let target = clamp(RangeLensSpan(start: (geometry.slot(at: x) - width / 2).rounded(), end: 0), width: width, n: n)
            guard target != span else { return }
            withAnimation(motion.snap) { span = target }
            tick += 1
            write(target, model: model)
        case .doubleTapped:
            toggleAll(model: model, announce: false)
        }
    }

    /// Where the lens is drawn for a finger at `x`: under it, stopped firmly at the minimum span, rubber-banded past
    /// either end of the series.
    private func tracked(_ grab: RangeLensGrab, x: CGFloat, geometry: RangeLensGeometry, n: Double) -> (span: RangeLensSpan, limited: Bool) {
        let delta = Double((x - grab.x) / geometry.slot)
        let origin = grab.origin
        let minimum = min(Double(minimumSpan), n)
        let band = { (over: Double) -> Double in
            Double(PieceMotion.rubberBand(CGFloat(over) * geometry.slot, limit: 28)) / Double(geometry.slot)
        }
        switch grab.part {
        case .body:
            let width = origin.end - origin.start
            var start = origin.start + delta
            var limited = false
            if start < 0 {
                start = band(start)
                limited = true
            } else if start + width > n {
                start = n - width + band(start + width - n)
                limited = true
            }
            return (RangeLensSpan(start: start, end: start + width), limited)
        case .lower:
            var start = origin.start + delta
            var limited = false
            if start > origin.end - minimum {
                start = origin.end - minimum
                limited = true
            } else if start < 0 {
                start = band(start)
                limited = true
            }
            return (RangeLensSpan(start: start, end: origin.end), limited)
        case .upper:
            var end = origin.end + delta
            var limited = false
            if end < origin.start + minimum {
                end = origin.start + minimum
                limited = true
            } else if end > n {
                end = n + band(end - n)
                limited = true
            }
            return (RangeLensSpan(start: origin.start, end: end), limited)
        }
    }

    /// Let go: the lens coasts a little with the finger's speed, lands on whole samples inside the series, and the
    /// selection takes its final value once.
    private func release(velocity: CGFloat, geometry: RangeLensGeometry, model: RangeLensModel) {
        guard let held = grab?.part else { return }
        let n = Double(model.count)
        let minimum = min(Double(minimumSpan), n)
        let slots = Double(PieceMotion.project(0, velocity: velocity) / geometry.slot)
        var target = span
        let from: Double
        let to: Double
        switch held {
        case .body:
            let width = (span.end - span.start).rounded()
            target = clamp(RangeLensSpan(start: (span.start + slots).rounded(), end: 0), width: max(width, minimum), n: n)
            from = span.start
            to = target.start
        case .lower:
            let start = min(max((span.start + slots).rounded(), 0), (span.end.rounded()) - minimum)
            target = RangeLensSpan(start: start, end: span.end.rounded())
            from = span.start
            to = target.start
        case .upper:
            let end = max(min((span.end + slots).rounded(), n), span.start.rounded() + minimum)
            target = RangeLensSpan(start: span.start.rounded(), end: end)
            from = span.end
            to = target.end
        }
        let settle = motion.settle(velocity: velocity, from: CGFloat(from) * geometry.slot, to: CGFloat(to) * geometry.slot)
        withAnimation(motion.release) { grab = nil }
        withAnimation(settle) { span = target }
        atLimit = false
        if model.bucket(edge(of: target, part: held, n: model.count)) != lastBucket { tick += 1 }
        lastBucket = nil
        write(target, model: model)
    }

    private func edge(of span: RangeLensSpan, part: RangeLensPart, n: Int) -> Int {
        part == .upper ? min(max(Int(span.end.rounded()) - 1, 0), n - 1) : min(max(Int(span.start.rounded()), 0), n - 1)
    }

    private func clamp(_ proposed: RangeLensSpan, width: Double, n: Double) -> RangeLensSpan {
        let width = min(max(width, 1), n)
        let start = min(max(proposed.start, 0), n - width)
        return RangeLensSpan(start: start, end: start + width)
    }

    /// Writes the window the lens rests on, once it changes, so the caller's state stays true while a finger moves.
    private func write(_ span: RangeLensSpan, model: RangeLensModel) {
        guard let window = model.window(of: span, minimum: minimumSpan) else { return }
        let range = model.dates[window.lowerBound]...model.dates[window.upperBound]
        guard range != selection else { return }
        written = range
        selection = range
    }

    // MARK: Steps (VoiceOver, keyboard)

    private func isAll(_ model: RangeLensModel) -> Bool {
        span.start <= 0 && span.end >= Double(model.count)
    }

    private func move(by step: Int, model: RangeLensModel, announce: Bool) {
        guard model.count > 1 else { return }
        let n = Double(model.count)
        let width = (span.end - span.start).rounded()
        let target = clamp(RangeLensSpan(start: span.start.rounded() + Double(step), end: 0), width: width, n: n)
        guard target != span else { return }
        withAnimation(motion.snap) { span = target }
        tick += 1
        write(target, model: model)
        if announce, let window = model.window(of: target, minimum: minimumSpan) {
            AccessibilityNotification.Announcement(lensValue(model, window)).post()
        }
    }

    /// Widens or narrows from the window's start, keeping its end, so a window that ends today keeps ending today.
    private func resize(by step: Int, model: RangeLensModel) {
        guard model.count > 1 else { return }
        let n = Double(model.count)
        let minimum = min(Double(minimumSpan), n)
        var start = min(max(span.start.rounded() - Double(step), 0), span.end.rounded() - minimum)
        var end = span.end.rounded()
        // Already at the start of the series: widen toward the end instead.
        if step > 0, start == span.start.rounded() { end = min(end + Double(step), n) }
        start = max(start, 0)
        let target = RangeLensSpan(start: start, end: end)
        guard target != span else { return }
        withAnimation(motion.morph) { span = target }
        tick += 1
        write(target, model: model)
        if let window = model.window(of: target, minimum: minimumSpan) {
            AccessibilityNotification.Announcement(lensValue(model, window)).post()
        }
    }

    /// Shows the whole series, and from there goes back to the window before it.
    private func toggleAll(model: RangeLensModel, announce: Bool) {
        guard model.count > 1 else { return }
        let all = RangeLensSpan(start: 0, end: Double(model.count))
        let target: RangeLensSpan
        if isAll(model) {
            guard let previous else { return }
            target = previous
            self.previous = nil
        } else {
            previous = RangeLensSpan(start: span.start.rounded(), end: span.end.rounded())
            target = all
        }
        withAnimation(motion.morph) { span = target }
        grabTick += 1
        write(target, model: model)
        if announce, let window = model.window(of: target, minimum: minimumSpan) {
            AccessibilityNotification.Announcement(lensValue(model, window)).post()
        }
    }

    // MARK: Spoken

    private func readoutSentence(_ model: RangeLensModel, _ window: ClosedRange<Int>) -> String {
        let value = valueLabel(model.value(summary, in: window))
        let range = model.spokenRange(window, calendar: calendar, locale: locale)
        if let unit, !unit.isEmpty {
            return String(localized: "\(summaryTitle) \(value) \(unit), \(range)", comment: "Range lens VoiceOver readout, as in Average 8,412 steps a day, 3 March to 14 April")
        }
        return String(localized: "\(summaryTitle) \(value), \(range)", comment: "Range lens VoiceOver readout without a unit, as in Average 8,412, 3 March to 14 April")
    }

    private func lensValue(_ model: RangeLensModel, _ window: ClosedRange<Int>) -> String {
        let range = model.spokenRange(window, calendar: calendar, locale: locale)
        let value = valueLabel(model.value(summary, in: window))
        if let unit, !unit.isEmpty {
            return String(localized: "\(range), \(summaryTitle) \(value) \(unit)", comment: "Range lens VoiceOver value, as in 3 March to 14 April, Average 8,412 steps a day")
        }
        return String(localized: "\(range), \(summaryTitle) \(value)", comment: "Range lens VoiceOver value without a unit, as in 3 March to 14 April, Average 8,412")
    }
}

// MARK: - Model

/// The lens in samples: `start` is the leading edge of the first sample in the window, `end` the trailing edge of
/// the last, so a window of one sample is one wide.
private struct RangeLensSpan: Equatable {
    var start: Double
    var end: Double
}

private enum RangeLensPart: Equatable {
    case lower, body, upper
}

private struct RangeLensGrab: Equatable {
    var part: RangeLensPart
    var origin: RangeLensSpan
    /// Where the finger came down, in points along the time axis.
    var x: CGFloat
}

/// The series, sorted, with running sums for constant-time averages and the marks the strip labels.
private struct RangeLensModel {
    struct Key: Equatable {
        var samples: [RangeLens.Sample]
        var calendar: Calendar
        var locale: Locale
    }

    struct Mark {
        var index: Int
        var text: String
        /// The mark names a year, so a thinned row keeps it.
        var isYear = false
    }

    var revision = 0
    let dates: [Date]
    let values: [Double]
    /// `prefix[i]` is the sum of the first `i` values.
    let prefix: [Double]
    let maximum: Double
    /// The haptic buckets: a week for daily samples, a month for weekly ones, a year for monthly ones, a day for
    /// anything finer. A tick plays when the moving edge crosses into another bucket.
    let buckets: [Int]
    /// How far a VoiceOver or keyboard step moves the lens: a bucket's worth of samples.
    let step: Int
    let marks: [Mark]
    /// Whether the series spans more than one calendar year, so dates carry their year.
    let latestYear: Int

    var count: Int { values.count }

    init(_ key: Key) {
        let sorted = key.samples.enumerated().sorted { $0.element.date == $1.element.date ? $0.offset < $1.offset : $0.element.date < $1.element.date }.map(\.element)
        dates = sorted.map(\.date)
        values = sorted.map { $0.value.isFinite ? max($0.value, 0) : 0 }
        var sums = [0.0]
        sums.reserveCapacity(values.count + 1)
        for value in values { sums.append(sums[sums.count - 1] + value) }
        prefix = sums
        maximum = values.max() ?? 0
        let calendar = key.calendar

        // How far apart the samples are, from the median gap, decides the ticks, the steps and the marks.
        var gaps = zip(dates.dropFirst(), dates).map { $0.timeIntervalSince($1) }
        gaps.sort()
        let gap = gaps.isEmpty ? 86_400 : gaps[gaps.count / 2]
        let day = 86_400.0
        let bucketUnit: Calendar.Component
        let perStep: Int
        switch gap {
        case ..<(day * 0.5): bucketUnit = .day; perStep = max(Int((day / max(gap, 1)).rounded()), 1)
        case ..<(day * 3): bucketUnit = .weekOfYear; perStep = 7
        case ..<(day * 20): bucketUnit = .month; perStep = 4
        default: bucketUnit = .year; perStep = 3
        }
        step = perStep
        buckets = dates.map { date in
            let start = calendar.dateInterval(of: bucketUnit, for: date)?.start ?? date
            return Int(start.timeIntervalSinceReferenceDate / 3_600)
        }

        // Marks under the strip: years for long series, months, then weeks, then days.
        let total = (dates.last ?? .now).timeIntervalSince(dates.first ?? .now)
        let markUnit: Calendar.Component = total > day * 800 ? .year : total > day * 62 ? .month : total > day * 6 ? .weekOfYear : .day
        let years = Set(dates.map { calendar.component(.year, from: $0) })
        var found: [Mark] = []
        var last: Date?
        for (index, date) in dates.enumerated() {
            let start = calendar.dateInterval(of: markUnit, for: date)?.start ?? date
            defer { last = start }
            guard let last, last != start else { continue }
            var text: String
            var isYear = false
            switch markUnit {
            case .year:
                text = date.formatted(.dateTime.year().locale(key.locale))
                isYear = true
            case .month:
                // January names its year when the series crosses one, so the strip says where it is.
                isYear = calendar.component(.month, from: date) == 1 && years.count > 1
                text = isYear ? date.formatted(.dateTime.year().locale(key.locale)) : date.formatted(.dateTime.month(.abbreviated).locale(key.locale))
            case .weekOfYear: text = date.formatted(.dateTime.day().month(.abbreviated).locale(key.locale))
            default: text = date.formatted(.dateTime.weekday(.abbreviated).locale(key.locale))
            }
            found.append(Mark(index: index, text: text, isYear: isYear))
        }
        marks = found
        latestYear = dates.last.map { calendar.component(.year, from: $0) } ?? 0
    }

    /// The span the lens draws for a selection: from its first sample on or after the lower date to its last on or
    /// before the upper date, at least `minimum` wide, clamped to the series.
    func span(for range: ClosedRange<Date>, minimum: Int) -> RangeLensSpan {
        let n = count
        guard n > 0 else { return RangeLensSpan(start: 0, end: 0) }
        var lo = dates.firstIndex { $0 >= range.lowerBound } ?? (n - 1)
        var hi = dates.lastIndex { $0 <= range.upperBound } ?? 0
        if hi < lo { hi = lo }
        let width = min(max(minimum, 1), n)
        if hi - lo + 1 < width {
            hi = min(lo + width - 1, n - 1)
            lo = max(hi - width + 1, 0)
        }
        return RangeLensSpan(start: Double(lo), end: Double(hi + 1))
    }

    /// The whole samples a span rests on.
    func window(of span: RangeLensSpan, minimum: Int) -> ClosedRange<Int>? {
        let n = count
        guard n > 0 else { return nil }
        var lo = min(max(Int(span.start.rounded()), 0), n - 1)
        var hi = min(max(Int(span.end.rounded()) - 1, lo), n - 1)
        let width = min(max(minimum, 1), n)
        if hi - lo + 1 < width {
            hi = min(lo + width - 1, n - 1)
            lo = max(hi - width + 1, 0)
        }
        return lo...hi
    }

    func value(_ summary: RangeLens.Summary, in window: ClosedRange<Int>) -> Double {
        switch summary {
        case .average: (prefix[window.upperBound + 1] - prefix[window.lowerBound]) / Double(window.count)
        case .total: prefix[window.upperBound + 1] - prefix[window.lowerBound]
        case .maximum: values[window].max() ?? 0
        case .minimum: values[window].min() ?? 0
        }
    }

    func peak(_ window: ClosedRange<Int>) -> Double {
        values[window].max() ?? 0
    }

    /// The mean over a fractional span, each sample weighted by how much of it the span covers, so the average
    /// rule glides with the bars instead of stepping.
    func mean(from start: Double, to end: Double) -> Double {
        let n = Double(count)
        let a = min(max(start, 0), n), b = min(max(end, 0), n)
        guard b - a > 0.0001 else { return 0 }
        func cumulative(_ x: Double) -> Double {
            let whole = Int(x.rounded(.down))
            guard whole < count else { return prefix[count] }
            return prefix[whole] + values[whole] * (x - Double(whole))
        }
        return (cumulative(b) - cumulative(a)) / (b - a)
    }

    func bucket(_ index: Int) -> Int? {
        buckets.indices.contains(index) ? buckets[index] : nil
    }

    private func carriesYear(_ window: ClosedRange<Int>, calendar: Calendar) -> Bool {
        let first = calendar.component(.year, from: dates[window.lowerBound])
        let last = calendar.component(.year, from: dates[window.upperBound])
        return first != last || last != latestYear
    }

    /// "3 Mar – 14 Apr", with the year when the window crosses one or lies in an earlier year.
    func dateRange(_ window: ClosedRange<Int>, calendar: Calendar, locale: Locale) -> String {
        let first = dates[window.lowerBound], last = dates[window.upperBound]
        var style = Date.IntervalFormatStyle(locale: locale, calendar: calendar).day().month(.abbreviated)
        if carriesYear(window, calendar: calendar) { style = style.year() }
        guard first < last else {
            var single = Date.FormatStyle(locale: locale, calendar: calendar).day().month(.abbreviated)
            if carriesYear(window, calendar: calendar) { single = single.year() }
            return first.formatted(single)
        }
        return (first..<last).formatted(style)
    }

    /// "3 March to 14 April", for VoiceOver, which reads a dash between dates unevenly.
    func spokenRange(_ window: ClosedRange<Int>, calendar: Calendar, locale: Locale) -> String {
        var style = Date.FormatStyle(locale: locale, calendar: calendar).day().month(.wide)
        if carriesYear(window, calendar: calendar) { style = style.year() }
        let first = dates[window.lowerBound].formatted(style), last = dates[window.upperBound].formatted(style)
        guard first != last else { return first }
        return String(localized: "\(first) to \(last)", comment: "Range lens VoiceOver date range, as in 3 March to 14 April")
    }
}

/// Keeps the last model, so a drag step (which re-runs `body`) never sorts or sums the series again.
@MainActor
private final class RangeLensCache {
    private var key: RangeLensModel.Key?
    private var model: RangeLensModel?
    private var revision = 0

    nonisolated init() {}

    func model(for key: RangeLensModel.Key) -> RangeLensModel {
        if let model, self.key == key { return model }
        revision += 1
        var next = RangeLensModel(key)
        next.revision = revision
        self.key = key
        model = next
        return next
    }
}

/// The detail chart's ceiling and the step between its gridlines. The ceiling follows the window's highest sample
/// with a little headroom, so the bars always fill the chart and it rescales as the window moves; the gridlines stay
/// on round values (5,000 apart for a peak of 13,240) and slide as it does.
private struct RangeLensScale: Equatable {
    var top: Double
    var step: Double

    init(peak: Double) {
        guard peak.isFinite, peak > 0 else {
            top = 1
            step = 0
            return
        }
        top = peak * 1.08
        // The smallest round step (1, 2 or 5 of a power of ten) that leaves at most four gridlines.
        let magnitude = pow(10, floor(log10(top)))
        let top = top
        step = [0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10].map { $0 * magnitude }.first { top / $0 <= 4 } ?? magnitude * 10
    }
}

// MARK: - Geometry

/// The strip's layout along the time axis, in points from the leading end: the bars sit inside the room the
/// handles need at either end, one slot per sample.
private struct RangeLensGeometry {
    var width: CGFloat
    var count: Int
    var handle: CGFloat
    var minimumLens: CGFloat

    var inset: CGFloat { handle + PieceLiquid.joined }
    var slot: CGFloat { count > 0 ? max(width - inset * 2, 1) / CGFloat(count) : 0 }

    func x(_ slot: Double) -> CGFloat { inset + CGFloat(slot) * self.slot }
    func slot(at x: CGFloat) -> Double { self.slot > 0 ? Double((x - inset) / self.slot) : 0 }

    /// A point on screen as a point along time: mirrored right to left.
    func time(_ x: CGFloat, rtl: Bool) -> CGFloat { rtl ? width - x : x }

    /// The slab's extent along time, never narrower than `minimumLens` so it stays a shape you can see and hold.
    func lens(_ span: RangeLensSpan) -> ClosedRange<CGFloat> {
        let lo = x(span.start), hi = x(span.end)
        let width = max(hi - lo, minimumLens)
        // Centred on the window, but kept on the track at either end (a band past the end may still pull it out).
        let floor = min(lo, inset), ceiling = max(hi, self.width - inset)
        let start = min(max((lo + hi) / 2 - width / 2, floor), ceiling - width)
        return start...(start + width)
    }

    /// Which part of the lens a finger at `x` takes: a handle within about 22 points of its edge (fewer on a narrow
    /// lens, so the slab keeps a middle), the slab between, or nothing off the lens.
    func part(at x: CGFloat, of span: RangeLensSpan) -> RangeLensPart? {
        let frame = lens(span)
        let reach = min(22, (frame.upperBound - frame.lowerBound) / 3)
        let outer = handle + PieceLiquid.joined + 12
        if x >= frame.lowerBound - outer, x <= frame.lowerBound + reach { return .lower }
        if x <= frame.upperBound + outer, x >= frame.upperBound - reach { return .upper }
        if x > frame.lowerBound, x < frame.upperBound { return .body }
        return nil
    }
}

/// One value interpolated frame by frame and handed to its content. Nested, two of these keep two springs apart:
/// the ceiling rescales on the value role while the window pans under the finger or lands on its own spring.
private struct RangeLensAnimated<Content: View>: View, Animatable {
    var value: Double
    @ViewBuilder let content: (Double) -> Content

    nonisolated var animatableData: Double {
        get { value }
        set { value = newValue }
    }

    var body: some View { content(value) }
}

/// The lens's two edges, interpolated together, so the bars, their colors and the glass land on one spring.
private struct RangeLensAnimatedSpan<Content: View>: View, Animatable {
    var start: Double
    var end: Double
    @ViewBuilder let content: (Double, Double) -> Content

    nonisolated var animatableData: AnimatablePair<Double, Double> {
        get { AnimatablePair(start, end) }
        set {
            start = newValue.first
            end = newValue.second
        }
    }

    var body: some View { content(start, end) }
}

// MARK: - Drawing

private enum RangeLensPainter {
    struct Detail {
        var model: RangeLensModel
        var step: Double
        var showsAverage: Bool
        var redacted: Bool
        var rtl: Bool
        var pad: CGFloat
        var axisSize: CGFloat
        var chipHeight: CGFloat
        var averageMark: String
        var displayScale: CGFloat
        var text: Color
        var label: Color
        var grid: Color
        var accent: Color
        var accentInk: Color
    }

    /// The window's bars, gridlines with their values, and the average rule with its chip, in one pass. `start` and
    /// `end` are the lens's live edges, so the bars slide by fractions of a sample while it moves; `top` is the
    /// animated ceiling.
    static func draw(_ input: Detail, start: Double, end: Double, top: Double, in context: inout GraphicsContext, size: CGSize) {
        let model = input.model
        let gutter = ceil(input.axisSize * 3) + 10
        let plotWidth = max(size.width - gutter, 1)
        // Room above for the chip and the top value, and below for the zero, which is centred on the baseline.
        let plot = CGRect(x: 0, y: input.pad, width: plotWidth, height: max(size.height - input.pad * 2, 1))
        let top = max(top, 0.0001)
        let hairline = 1 / max(input.displayScale, 1)
        let snap = { (y: CGFloat) -> CGFloat in (y * input.displayScale).rounded() / input.displayScale }
        let mirror = { (rect: CGRect) -> CGRect in
            input.rtl ? CGRect(x: size.width - rect.maxX, y: rect.minY, width: rect.width, height: rect.height) : rect
        }
        let yOf = { (value: Double) -> CGFloat in plot.maxY - CGFloat(value / top) * plot.height }

        // The average and its chip, placed first so the gridline value under the chip can step aside.
        let average = model.count > 0 && input.showsAverage ? model.mean(from: start, to: end) : nil
        let chipText = context.resolve(Text(input.averageMark).font(.system(size: input.axisSize, weight: .semibold)).foregroundStyle(input.accentInk))
        let chipSize = chipText.measure(in: CGSize(width: 200, height: 100))
        let chipWidth = max(ceil(chipSize.width) + 12, input.chipHeight)
        var chipY: CGFloat?
        if let average, average > 0 {
            chipY = min(max(yOf(average), plot.minY + input.chipHeight / 2 - input.pad + 2), plot.maxY - input.chipHeight / 2)
        }

        // Gridlines and their values on the axis side.
        var lines = Path()
        if input.step > 0 {
            var tick = 0.0
            while tick <= top * 1.0001 + input.step {
                let y = yOf(tick)
                if y >= plot.minY - 0.5, tick > 0 {
                    lines.addRect(mirror(CGRect(x: 0, y: snap(y) - hairline / 2, width: plotWidth, height: hairline)))
                }
                if y >= plot.minY - 0.5, !input.redacted, chipY.map({ abs($0 - y) > input.chipHeight * 0.8 }) ?? true {
                    let label = context.resolve(Text(tick.formatted(.number.notation(.compactName))).font(.system(size: input.axisSize, weight: .semibold)).monospacedDigit().foregroundStyle(input.label))
                    let x = plotWidth + 8
                    context.draw(label, at: CGPoint(x: input.rtl ? size.width - x : x, y: y), anchor: input.rtl ? .trailing : .leading)
                }
                tick += input.step
            }
        }
        if input.step <= 0, !input.redacted {
            // Nothing above zero: the baseline still names its value.
            let zero = context.resolve(Text(0.0.formatted(.number)).font(.system(size: input.axisSize, weight: .semibold)).monospacedDigit().foregroundStyle(input.label))
            let x = plotWidth + 8
            context.draw(zero, at: CGPoint(x: input.rtl ? size.width - x : x, y: plot.maxY), anchor: input.rtl ? .trailing : .leading)
        }
        lines.addRect(mirror(CGRect(x: 0, y: snap(plot.maxY) - hairline, width: plotWidth, height: hairline)))
        context.fill(lines, with: .color(input.grid))

        // The bars that fall in the window, sliding by fractions of a sample, clipped to the plot.
        let visible = max(end - start, 0.0001)
        let slot = plotWidth / CGFloat(visible)
        // Wide slots keep a gap; below a point and a half the bars touch, so a year reads as one clean silhouette.
        let barWidth = slot < 1.5 ? slot : min(slot * (slot > 6 ? 0.62 : slot > 2.5 ? 0.7 : 0.8), 24)
        let radius = min(barWidth / 2, 3)
        var bars = Path()
        if model.count > 0 {
            let first = max(Int(start.rounded(.down)), 0)
            let last = min(Int(end.rounded(.up)), model.count)
            if first < last {
                for index in first..<last {
                    let value = model.values[index]
                    guard value > 0 else { continue }
                    let x = CGFloat(Double(index) - start) * slot + (slot - barWidth) / 2
                    let height = max(CGFloat(value / top) * plot.height, 1)
                    let rect = CGRect(x: x, y: plot.maxY - height, width: barWidth, height: height + radius)
                    // Hairline bars are plain rects: a rounded rect narrower than a point draws as a smear.
                    if barWidth < 2.5 {
                        bars.addRect(mirror(rect))
                    } else {
                        bars.addRoundedRect(in: mirror(rect), cornerSize: CGSize(width: radius, height: radius), style: .continuous)
                    }
                }
            }
        }
        context.drawLayer { layer in
            layer.clip(to: Path(mirror(CGRect(x: 0, y: 0, width: plotWidth, height: plot.maxY))))
            layer.fill(bars, with: .color(input.redacted ? input.grid : input.text))
        }

        // The average: a red dashed rule across the plot and its chip on the axis.
        if let average, average > 0, let chipY {
            let y = snap(yOf(average))
            var rule = Path()
            let a = mirror(CGRect(x: 0, y: y, width: plotWidth, height: 0))
            rule.move(to: CGPoint(x: a.minX, y: y))
            rule.addLine(to: CGPoint(x: a.maxX, y: y))
            context.stroke(rule, with: .color(input.accent), style: StrokeStyle(lineWidth: 1.5, lineCap: .round, dash: [3, 4]))
            let chip = mirror(CGRect(x: plotWidth + 4, y: chipY - input.chipHeight / 2, width: chipWidth, height: input.chipHeight))
            context.fill(Path(roundedRect: chip, cornerRadius: input.chipHeight / 2, style: .continuous), with: .color(input.accent))
            context.draw(chipText, at: CGPoint(x: chip.midX, y: chip.midY), anchor: .center)
        }
    }

    struct Strip {
        var model: RangeLensModel
        var geometry: RangeLensGeometry
        var rtl: Bool
        var rowHeight: CGFloat
        var barInset: CGFloat
        var axisSize: CGFloat
        var redacted: Bool
        var inside: Color
        var outside: Color
        var label: Color
        var baseline: Color
        var track: Color
    }

    enum Layer { case under, over }

    /// Every sample as a thin bar on one scale, and the marks under them. The `under` layer draws the bars outside
    /// the lens, dim, and the marks; the `over` layer draws the bars inside it, bright, on the glass.
    static func drawStrip(_ input: Strip, layer: Layer, start: Double, end: Double, in context: inout GraphicsContext, size: CGSize) {
        let model = input.model
        let g = input.geometry
        let floor = input.rowHeight - input.barInset
        let room = input.rowHeight - input.barInset * 2
        let mirror = { (rect: CGRect) -> CGRect in
            input.rtl ? CGRect(x: size.width - rect.maxX, y: rect.minY, width: rect.width, height: rect.height) : rect
        }
        // The track the lens rides: the strip's whole width, a quiet solid step off the ground.
        if layer == .under {
            context.fill(Path(roundedRect: CGRect(x: 0, y: 0, width: size.width, height: input.rowHeight), cornerRadius: 12, style: .continuous), with: .color(input.track))
        }
        guard model.count > 0, g.slot > 0 else { return }
        let barWidth = min(max(g.slot * (g.slot > 3 ? 0.62 : 0.72), 0.5), 8)
        let radius = min(barWidth / 2, 1.5)
        let peak = max(model.maximum, 0.0001)
        var bars = Path()
        for index in 0..<model.count {
            let value = model.values[index]
            let height = value > 0 ? max(CGFloat(value / peak) * room, 1.5) : 1
            let x = g.x(Double(index)) + (g.slot - barWidth) / 2
            let rect = mirror(CGRect(x: x, y: floor - height, width: barWidth, height: height))
            let center = Double(index) + 0.5
            let inside = center > start && center < end
            guard inside == (layer == .over) else { continue }
            if barWidth < 2.5 {
                bars.addRect(rect)
            } else {
                bars.addRoundedRect(in: rect, cornerSize: CGSize(width: radius, height: radius))
            }
        }
        guard layer == .under else {
            context.fill(bars, with: .color(input.redacted ? input.baseline : input.inside))
            return
        }
        context.fill(bars, with: .color(input.redacted ? input.baseline : input.outside))

        // Marks at their boundaries, every one or every second, third... whichever is the first that never crowds,
        // so they stay evenly spaced; the stride keeps the mark that names a new year.
        guard !input.redacted, !model.marks.isEmpty else { return }
        let labels = model.marks.map { mark in
            let text = context.resolve(Text(mark.text).font(.system(size: input.axisSize, weight: .semibold)).foregroundStyle(input.label))
            return (x: g.x(Double(mark.index)), width: text.measure(in: CGSize(width: 200, height: 60)).width, text: text)
        }
        let anchor = model.marks.firstIndex { $0.isYear } ?? 0
        var kept: [Int] = []
        for stride in 1...max(labels.count, 1) {
            let phase = anchor % stride
            let picks = labels.indices.filter { $0 % stride == phase && labels[$0].x + labels[$0].width <= size.width - 2 }
            let fits = zip(picks, picks.dropFirst()).allSatisfy { labels[$0].x + labels[$0].width + 14 <= labels[$1].x }
            if fits {
                kept = picks
                break
            }
        }
        let y = input.rowHeight + 6
        for index in kept {
            let label = labels[index]
            context.draw(label.text, at: CGPoint(x: input.rtl ? size.width - label.x : label.x, y: y), anchor: input.rtl ? .topTrailing : .topLeading)
        }
    }
}

// MARK: - Touch surface

private enum RangeLensTouchEvent {
    /// A drag begins where the finger came down: from a horizontal pan, or from a press held still (`pressed`).
    case began(CGPoint, pressed: Bool)
    case moved(CGPoint)
    /// Velocity along x in points per second.
    case ended(CGPoint, velocity: CGFloat)
    case cancelled
    case tapped(CGPoint)
    case doubleTapped(CGPoint)
}

/// UIKit recognizers over the strip, because SwiftUI has no drag that leaves an enclosing scroll view free to
/// scroll: any drag gesture there claims the touch. The pan begins only for a mostly horizontal drag, and the press
/// only for a finger held still, so a vertical swipe goes to the scroll view or sheet untouched. A single tap waits
/// for the double tap to fail.
private struct RangeLensTouchSurface: UIViewRepresentable {
    var isEnabled: Bool
    var onEvent: (RangeLensTouchEvent) -> Void

    func makeCoordinator() -> Coordinator { Coordinator() }

    func makeUIView(context: Context) -> UIView {
        let view = UIView()
        view.backgroundColor = .clear
        view.isAccessibilityElement = false
        view.accessibilityElementsHidden = true
        let coordinator = context.coordinator
        let press = UILongPressGestureRecognizer(target: coordinator, action: #selector(Coordinator.drag(_:)))
        press.minimumPressDuration = 0.28
        press.allowableMovement = 8
        let pan = UIPanGestureRecognizer(target: coordinator, action: #selector(Coordinator.drag(_:)))
        pan.delegate = coordinator
        let double = UITapGestureRecognizer(target: coordinator, action: #selector(Coordinator.doubleTap(_:)))
        double.numberOfTapsRequired = 2
        let tap = UITapGestureRecognizer(target: coordinator, action: #selector(Coordinator.tap(_:)))
        tap.require(toFail: double)
        for recognizer in [press, pan, double, tap] { view.addGestureRecognizer(recognizer) }
        coordinator.press = press
        return view
    }

    func updateUIView(_ view: UIView, context: Context) {
        context.coordinator.onEvent = onEvent
        view.isUserInteractionEnabled = isEnabled
    }

    @MainActor
    final class Coordinator: NSObject, UIGestureRecognizerDelegate {
        var onEvent: (RangeLensTouchEvent) -> Void = { _ in }
        weak var press: UILongPressGestureRecognizer?
        /// Recent finger positions, for the velocity of a drag that began as a press.
        private var trail: [(time: TimeInterval, x: CGFloat)] = []

        @objc func drag(_ recognizer: UIGestureRecognizer) {
            let point = recognizer.location(in: recognizer.view)
            switch recognizer.state {
            case .began:
                var origin = point
                if let pan = recognizer as? UIPanGestureRecognizer {
                    let moved = pan.translation(in: pan.view)
                    origin = CGPoint(x: point.x - moved.x, y: point.y - moved.y)
                }
                trail = [(CACurrentMediaTime(), point.x)]
                onEvent(.began(origin, pressed: recognizer === press))
                // The pan begins after a few points of travel; catch up to the finger at once.
                if origin != point { onEvent(.moved(point)) }
            case .changed:
                trail.append((CACurrentMediaTime(), point.x))
                if trail.count > 6 { trail.removeFirst(trail.count - 6) }
                onEvent(.moved(point))
            case .ended:
                onEvent(.ended(point, velocity: velocity(of: recognizer)))
            case .cancelled, .failed:
                onEvent(.cancelled)
            default:
                break
            }
        }

        @objc func tap(_ recognizer: UITapGestureRecognizer) {
            onEvent(.tapped(recognizer.location(in: recognizer.view)))
        }

        @objc func doubleTap(_ recognizer: UITapGestureRecognizer) {
            onEvent(.doubleTapped(recognizer.location(in: recognizer.view)))
        }

        private func velocity(of recognizer: UIGestureRecognizer) -> CGFloat {
            if let pan = recognizer as? UIPanGestureRecognizer { return pan.velocity(in: pan.view).x }
            // A press reports no velocity; read it off the last few positions, unless the finger has rested.
            guard let last = trail.last, let first = trail.first, CACurrentMediaTime() - last.time < 0.08, last.time - first.time > 0.001 else { return 0 }
            return (last.x - first.x) / CGFloat(last.time - first.time)
        }

        /// The pan only starts for a mostly horizontal drag, so vertical swipes stay with the screen.
        func gestureRecognizerShouldBegin(_ recognizer: UIGestureRecognizer) -> Bool {
            guard let pan = recognizer as? UIPanGestureRecognizer else { return true }
            let velocity = pan.velocity(in: pan.view)
            return abs(velocity.x) > abs(velocity.y)
        }
    }
}

/// A year of daily steps ending today: weekends higher, a summer rise, a holiday dip at the turn of the year and the
/// odd rest day. The same every time.
private enum RangeLensSample {
    static func steps(days: Int = 365, calendar: Calendar = .current, now: Date = .now) -> [RangeLens.Sample] {
        let today = calendar.startOfDay(for: now)
        return (0..<days).compactMap { k in
            guard let date = calendar.date(byAdding: .day, value: k - (days - 1), to: today) else { return nil }
            return RangeLens.Sample(date: date, value: value(k, date: date, calendar: calendar))
        }
    }

    static func value(_ k: Int, date: Date, calendar: Calendar) -> Double {
        let noise = Double((k * 7_919 + 13) % 101) / 100
        let rest = Double((k * 4_021 + 71) % 97) / 96
        let weekday = calendar.component(.weekday, from: date)
        let month = calendar.component(.month, from: date)
        let day = calendar.component(.day, from: date)
        let dayOfYear = Double(calendar.ordinality(of: .day, in: .year, for: date) ?? 1)
        let summer = exp(-pow((dayOfYear - 200) / 48, 2))
        var steps = 7_400 + 2_300 * summer + (weekday == 7 ? 2_800 : weekday == 1 ? 1_900 : 0) + (noise - 0.5) * 2_800
        if (month == 12 && day >= 23) || (month == 1 && day <= 1) { steps *= 0.5 }
        if rest < 0.05 { steps = 2_400 + noise * 900 }
        return max(steps, 600).rounded()
    }
}

// MARK: - Piece motion
//
// The SwiftPieces motion language: shared spring tokens and gesture physics, with the same values in every
// piece. Each piece carries a copy of only the parts it uses, so this file stands alone. Generated from
// registry/foundation/PieceMotion.swift in the SwiftPieces repo; edit it there, not here.
// swiftpieces-motion: 1.1.0 (core, momentum, rubberBand)

/// The SwiftPieces motion language: five spring tiers, and a named role for every moment a piece moves,
/// each with its Reduce Motion substitute.
///
/// Build one from the environment, `PieceMotion(reduceMotion: reduceMotion)`, and pick the role that names
/// what just happened. Never animate the tracking of a finger: set gesture state directly in `onChanged`, so the
/// surface stays under the finger, and spring only the release.
private struct PieceMotion {
    var reduceMotion = false

    // Tiers. Overshoot and settle times are measured from rest with SwiftUI's Spring.
    /// No overshoot, 90% in about 90ms. A press arriving under the finger.
    static var tight: Spring { Spring(duration: 0.14, bounce: 0) }
    /// About 2.8% overshoot, 90% in about 140ms. Snapping to a detent, page or segment.
    static var responsive: Spring { Spring(duration: 0.32, bounce: 0.25) }
    /// About 8.4% overshoot. Visible give: a release, a return from past an edge, a drag settling home.
    static var elastic: Spring { Spring(duration: 0.42, bounce: 0.38) }
    /// About 15% overshoot. A resolved action landing. At most once per interaction.
    static var expressive: Spring { Spring(duration: 0.48, bounce: 0.48) }
    /// About 1.5% overshoot, unhurried. Opening large surfaces and ambient change.
    static var calm: Spring { Spring(duration: 0.5, bounce: 0.2) }

    /// Reduce Motion: settles become this short spring with no overshoot.
    private static var still: Animation { .spring(duration: 0.25, bounce: 0) }

    // Roles.
    /// Touch-down. Starts on the same frame and never bounces under the finger.
    var press: Animation { .spring(Self.tight) }
    /// The finger lifts off a pressed surface, which springs back through rest.
    var release: Animation { reduceMotion ? Self.still : .spring(Self.elastic) }
    /// A dragged thing comes to rest. With a gesture's velocity, use `settle(velocity:from:to:)` instead.
    var settle: Animation { reduceMotion ? Self.still : .spring(Self.elastic) }
    /// Lands on a detent, page or segment.
    var snap: Animation { reduceMotion ? Self.still : .spring(Self.responsive) }
    /// A number, a chart value or anything else people read moves to its new value. Never overshoots, so it
    /// never shows a value that isn't true.
    var value: Animation { reduceMotion ? Self.still : .spring(duration: 0.35, bounce: 0) }
    /// Comes back from past a limit: a pull beyond the edge, a value pushed against its bound.
    var rebound: Animation { reduceMotion ? Self.still : .spring(Self.elastic) }
    /// A shape or container changes size, corner radius or form, with a little give at the end.
    var morph: Animation { reduceMotion ? Self.still : .spring(duration: 0.4, bounce: 0.2) }
    /// Something appears, opens or expands. Opening is a little slower than closing.
    var reveal: Animation { reduceMotion ? .easeOut(duration: 0.2) : .spring(Self.calm) }
    /// Something leaves, closes or collapses. Quick and firm, out of the way.
    var dismiss: Animation { reduceMotion ? .easeIn(duration: 0.18) : .spring(duration: 0.3, bounce: 0.08) }
    /// A resolved action lands: a check, a sent state, a reaction.
    var success: Animation { reduceMotion ? .easeOut(duration: 0.24) : .spring(Self.expressive) }
    /// A refused action. Firm, no wobble; `pieceShake` adds the movement.
    var error: Animation { reduceMotion ? .easeOut(duration: 0.2) : .spring(Self.responsive) }
    /// Slow ambient change. Loops themselves stop under Reduce Motion: check `allowsAmbient`.
    var ambient: Animation { reduceMotion ? .easeInOut(duration: 0.3) : .spring(Self.calm) }

    /// Loops, drifts, idle breathing and parallax run only when this is true.
    var allowsAmbient: Bool { !reduceMotion }

    /// A moving transition, or a plain fade under Reduce Motion.
    func transition(_ transition: AnyTransition) -> AnyTransition {
        reduceMotion ? .opacity : transition
    }

    /// For content replaced inside a container that stays put (a label, a glyph, a count): the old content
    /// blurs out as the new one sharpens in. A fade under Reduce Motion.
    @MainActor var swap: AnyTransition {
        reduceMotion ? .opacity : AnyTransition(.blurReplace)
    }
}

extension PieceMotion {
    /// Where a flick at `velocity` (pt/s) coasts to. 0.998 coasts like a scroll view; 0.99 suits detents.
    nonisolated static func project(_ position: CGFloat, velocity: CGFloat, decelerationRate: CGFloat = 0.99) -> CGFloat {
        position + velocity / 1000 * decelerationRate / (1 - decelerationRate)
    }

    /// The candidate closest to `value`.
    nonisolated static func nearest(_ value: CGFloat, in candidates: [CGFloat]) -> CGFloat {
        candidates.min { abs($0 - value) < abs($1 - value) } ?? value
    }

    /// A settle that leaves at the finger's speed (pt/s). One per axis, each on its own `.offset(x:)` / `.offset(y:)`:
    /// a spring takes one velocity. SwiftUI's own velocity carry-over is unreliable; this always carries it.
    func settle(velocity: CGFloat, from current: CGFloat, to target: CGFloat, spring: Spring = PieceMotion.elastic) -> Animation {
        let spring = reduceMotion ? Spring(duration: 0.25, bounce: 0) : spring
        let distance = target - current
        guard abs(distance) >= 1 else { return .spring(spring) }
        // In whole distances per second, capped near the spring's frequency: a hard flick adds give, not a slingshot.
        // At exactly the frequency a critically damped spring cannot pass its target, so Reduce Motion stops there.
        let cap = 2 * Double.pi / spring.duration * (reduceMotion ? 1 : 1.5)
        let relative = min(max(Double(velocity / distance), -cap), cap)
        return .interpolatingSpring(spring, initialVelocity: relative)
    }
}

extension PieceMotion {
    /// A scroll view's edge resistance for a pull `overshoot` points past a limit; never reaches `limit`.
    /// About 24 to 40 for thumbs and toggles, 60 to 120 for cards and sheets. Band the total pull, not deltas.
    nonisolated static func rubberBand(_ overshoot: CGFloat, limit: CGFloat, coefficient: CGFloat = 0.55) -> CGFloat {
        guard limit > 0, overshoot != 0 else { return 0 }
        let banded = (1 - 1 / (abs(overshoot) * coefficient / limit + 1)) * limit
        return overshoot < 0 ? -banded : banded
    }

    /// `value` inside `range` passes through unchanged; past either end it moves with rubber-band resistance.
    nonisolated static func rubberBand(_ value: CGFloat, in range: ClosedRange<CGFloat>, limit: CGFloat, coefficient: CGFloat = 0.55) -> CGFloat {
        if value < range.lowerBound { return range.lowerBound + rubberBand(value - range.lowerBound, limit: limit, coefficient: coefficient) }
        if value > range.upperBound { return range.upperBound + rubberBand(value - range.upperBound, limit: limit, coefficient: coefficient) }
        return value
    }
}

// and melt back into each other, and the frosted fallback before iOS 26. Each piece carries a copy of only
// the parts it uses, so this file stands alone. Generated from registry/foundation/PieceLiquid.swift in the

/// The liquid glass language: one merge distance, two rest gaps, and the springs a bubble leaves and comes home on.
///
/// Glass shapes inside one `PieceLiquidGroup` melt into each other through a neck when they come within `merge`
/// points. Parts of one control rest `joined`, inside that distance, so the neck holds; separate actions rest
/// `apart`, outside it, so they only goo while one buds out of, or melts back into, another.
private enum PieceLiquid {
    /// Glass shapes closer than this share a neck.
    static let merge: CGFloat = 20
    /// The gap between parts of one control (a stepper's buttons, a progress pill and its stop): the neck holds,
    /// short and smooth, about two thirds of the shapes' height at its waist. Joined parts read best at one height.
    static let joined: CGFloat = 4
    /// The gap between separate actions (menu items, confirm and cancel, chips): they rest as their own bubbles.
    static let apart: CGFloat = 26
    /// How far a bubble shrinks while it is home inside its parent.
    static let homeScale: CGFloat = 0.72

    /// A bubble leaving its parent: slow enough that the neck's stretch and snap read.
    static func split(reduceMotion: Bool) -> Animation {
        reduceMotion ? .easeOut(duration: 0.2) : .spring(duration: 0.62, bounce: 0.22)
    }

    /// A bubble going home. No bounce: a bounce would carry it out through the far side of its parent.
    static func home(reduceMotion: Bool) -> Animation {
        reduceMotion ? .easeIn(duration: 0.18) : .spring(duration: 0.5, bounce: 0)
    }
}

/// A group of glass shapes that merge into one liquid surface. On iOS 26 it is a `GlassEffectContainer`; before
/// that, and under Reduce Transparency, the shapes draw on their own and simply don't merge. `lift` adds the soft
/// shadow liquid glass floats on in light mode.
private struct PieceLiquidGroup<Content: View>: View {
    var spacing: CGFloat = PieceLiquid.merge
    var lift = true
    @ViewBuilder var content: Content
    @Environment(\.accessibilityReduceTransparency) private var reduceTransparency
    @Environment(\.colorScheme) private var colorScheme

    var body: some View {
        container
            .shadow(color: .black.opacity(lift && colorScheme == .light ? 0.07 : 0), radius: 18, y: 8)
    }

    @ViewBuilder private var container: some View {
        #if compiler(>=6.2)
        if #available(iOS 26, *), !reduceTransparency {
            GlassEffectContainer(spacing: spacing) { content }
        } else {
            content
        }
        #else
        content
        #endif
    }
}

/// One liquid glass shape: Liquid Glass on iOS 26, carrying `tint` as a solid colour and swelling under a press when
/// `interactive`; a frosted Material with a light rim and a soft shadow before that; a solid fill under Reduce
/// Transparency.
///
/// The tint is painted inside clear glass rather than tinting the glass. Tinted glass in a group bleeds its colour
/// through every neck as a smear, so a red button would glow into the white pill it is joined to; painted inside,
/// the colour stays crisp to the shape's edge, the necks between shapes are clear glass, and a tint change animates
/// like any colour (tinted glass snaps).
private struct PieceLiquidSurface<S: Shape>: ViewModifier {
    var shape: S
    var tint: Color?
    var interactive: Bool
    @Environment(\.accessibilityReduceTransparency) private var reduceTransparency
    @Environment(\.colorScheme) private var colorScheme
    @Environment(\.pieceLiquidScale) private var scale

    func body(content: Content) -> some View {
        #if compiler(>=6.2)
        if #available(iOS 26, *), !reduceTransparency {
            // Scaled as two parts, content and outline, about the same centre. A scaleEffect on a glass view inside a
            // GlassEffectContainer shrinks the glass but leaves what it carries full size, pinned to its corner.
            content
                .background { shape.fill(tint ?? .clear) }
                .scaleEffect(scale)
                .glassEffect(glass, in: shape.scale(scale))
        } else {
            fallback(content).scaleEffect(scale)
        }
        #else
        fallback(content).scaleEffect(scale)
        #endif
    }

    #if compiler(>=6.2)
    @available(iOS 26, *)
    private var glass: Glass {
        interactive ? Glass.regular.interactive() : .regular
    }
    #endif

    private func fallback(_ content: Content) -> some View {
        let dark = colorScheme == .dark
        return content
            .background {
                if reduceTransparency {
                    shape.fill(tint ?? (dark ? Color(white: 0.17) : Color(white: 0.97)))
                } else {
                    ZStack {
                        shape.fill(.regularMaterial)
                        if let tint { shape.fill(tint.opacity(0.88)) }
                    }
                }
            }
            .overlay { shape.stroke(Color.white.opacity(dark ? 0.14 : 0.7), lineWidth: 0.5) }
            .shadow(color: .black.opacity(dark ? 0.32 : 0.08), radius: 10, y: 5)
    }
}

private struct PieceLiquidScaleKey: EnvironmentKey {
    static let defaultValue: CGFloat = 1
}

private extension EnvironmentValues {
    /// How much the liquid shapes below are scaled, about their own centres. Nested scales multiply.
    var pieceLiquidScale: CGFloat {
        get { self[PieceLiquidScaleKey.self] }
        set { self[PieceLiquidScaleKey.self] = newValue }
    }
}

private extension View {
    /// Draws this view on a liquid glass `shape`. Put it inside a `PieceLiquidGroup` so it can merge with its neighbours.
    func pieceLiquid<S: Shape>(_ shape: S, tint: Color? = nil, interactive: Bool = true) -> some View {
        modifier(PieceLiquidSurface(shape: shape, tint: tint, interactive: interactive))
    }

    /// Scales the liquid glass shapes in this view, content and outline together, about their own centres. Use it
    /// instead of `scaleEffect` on a glass view (a press, a lift, a swell): inside a group a plain `scaleEffect` shrinks
    /// the glass but leaves its content full size and off centre. Animates like any other value.
    func pieceLiquidScale(_ scale: CGFloat) -> some View {
        transformEnvironment(\.pieceLiquidScale) { $0 *= scale }
    }
}
