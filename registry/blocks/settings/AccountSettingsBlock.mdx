## How it fits together

- The block is four groups on plain cards, top to bottom: the profile, Display, Notifications, and the way out. The cards are solid fills with continuous corners; the glass belongs to the controls inside them.
- The profile card shows the photo, or the initials on a tint the name picks (the same name always gets the same tint), with the name and email beside it. With `onEditProfile` set the whole card is one button with a small glass Edit pill as its affordance, and it sinks a little under the finger. When the name or email would be cut short beside the pill, a chevron takes its place.
- Display holds two controls. `GlassSegments` picks System, Light or Dark and writes `settings.appearance`. `ExpandingTrack` sets the text size between a small and a large "A", from 70% to 130% in steps of 10%; while it is held it buds the percentage above your finger. The sample line above it grows and shrinks with each step on a spring that never overshoots. It sits on the bottom of a box sized for 130%, so nothing under it moves while you drag, and the gap above the track leaves room for the readout bubble.
- Notifications are native `Toggle`s in the house red, one row per `NotificationSetting`, with hairlines between them. They write `isOn` straight into the bound value.
- Sign out is a plain row. While `onSignOut` runs it shows a spinner and ignores further taps.
- Deleting the account takes a `HoldToConfirm` hold: the red sweeps through the capsule from the trash puck, and letting go early drains it back. The line under it says what deleting removes. When the hold completes, the capsule turns sage with its check, the line changes to "Deleting your account…" with a spinner while `onDeleteAccount` runs, and then to the result. On success it reads "Your account is deleted." and the capsule stays locked. On failure it reads "Couldn't delete your account. Try again." with a butter mark, shakes once with the error haptic, and the hold works again. The capsule locks only after its own confirmation has played, so it turns sage before it greys.

## Wiring it to your app

Everything the person changes lands in one `AccountSettingsBlock.Settings` value. Keep it in your app's state, save it when it changes, and apply it where it takes effect. The actions are closures; the two async ones run on the main actor, and a plain synchronous closure works too.

```swift
@State private var settings = AccountSettingsBlock.Settings(
    appearance: .system,
    textSize: 1,
    notifications: [
        .init(id: "reminders", title: "Reminders", detail: "At the time you pick", isOn: true),
        .init(id: "summary", title: "Weekly summary", detail: "Sundays, by email", isOn: false),
    ]
)

var body: some View {
    ScrollView {
        AccountSettingsBlock(
            profile: .init(name: user.name, email: user.email, photo: user.photo),
            settings: $settings,
            onEditProfile: { showsProfileEditor = true },
            onSignOut: { await session.signOut() },
            onDeleteAccount: { try await api.deleteAccount() }
        )
        .padding(20)
    }
    .onChange(of: settings) { _, new in preferences.save(new) }
}
```

- Appearance: apply it once, at your app's root, with `.preferredColorScheme(settings.appearance.colorScheme)`. `.system` gives `nil`, which follows the system setting.
- Text size: `settings.textSize` is a multiple of your reading text's default size, so 1.2 means 20% larger. Multiply the size of the text people read by it, starting from a `@ScaledMetric` base so it still follows Dynamic Type: `.font(.system(size: bodySize * (settings.textSize ?? 1)))`. Leave controls and navigation at their system sizes. Set `textSize` to `nil` to hide the control if your app has no reading text.
- Notifications: each `NotificationSetting` has a stable `id` to save the choice under. Ask for notification permission when the person first turns one on, and register or cancel the matching schedule in `onChange`. An empty list hides the section.
- `onDeleteAccount` should throw if the request fails. After it returns, sign the person out and leave the screen; the block shows the success line until you do.
- Pass `nil` for `onEditProfile` to show the card without its affordance, and `nil` for `onSignOut` or `onDeleteAccount` to hide that control.

## Customizing

- `Style` sets the card fill, the label and secondary colors, the hairlines, the toggle tint, the success and warning marks, the ink on solid colors, the avatar tints and the card corner radius. `.standard` is the house palette in light and dark.
- The copy lives in the block as `Text` literals, so it goes through your string catalog: the section headers, the sample line, the consequence line and the result messages. Change the consequence line to say what your app actually removes.
- The text size range and step are `Settings.textSizes` and `Settings.textSizeStep`. The sample line's reserved height follows the top of the range.
- The appearance segments' titles and symbols come from `Appearance.title` and `Appearance.systemImage`. The symbols show only while every title fits beside its symbol.
- The hold uses `HoldToConfirm`'s house style: the signal fill, sage when confirmed, 1.2 seconds. Pass `duration:` or a `style:` in `hold(_:_:)` to change them.
- To add a section, follow `section(_:content:)`: a header over a card.

## Accessibility

- The profile card is one button that reads the name and email, with the hint "Opens your profile to edit it". Without `onEditProfile` it is one element that reads them.
- Section headers carry the header trait, so the rotor can jump between Display, Notifications and the rest.
- The appearance segments read as "Appearance" with each option selectable by name. The text size track is an adjustable element labelled "Text size" with its value as a percentage; swipe up or down to step it. The sample line is hidden from VoiceOver, since the value already says the size.
- Each notification toggle reads its title and detail with its state. The hold reads "Hold to delete account" with the hint "Touch and hold to confirm", and VoiceOver's activate action confirms it directly. The result of a deletion is announced.
- Dynamic Type: everything grows to the accessibility sizes. There the profile card stacks the photo, name, email and Edit pill, the segments drop their symbols, and rows grow to fit their text. The hold's title is one line that cannot wrap, so a narrow screen or a large text size gets "Delete account", and at the largest sizes that title stops growing at extra large rather than run out of the capsule.
- Touch targets are at least 44pt: the cards and rows are 52pt or taller, and the hold capsule is 60pt.
- Reduce Motion: the sample line steps on a short spring with no overshoot, the result line fades in place instead of blurring, the failure skips its shake and keeps its haptic and mark, and the pieces follow their own Reduce Motion behaviour. Reduce Transparency draws the Edit pill and the pieces' glass as solid shapes.
