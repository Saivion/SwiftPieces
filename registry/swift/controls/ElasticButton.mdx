## Notes

- It is a `ButtonStyle`, so it works on any `Button`: `.buttonStyle(.elastic)` adds only the motion, `.buttonStyle(.elastic(.signal))` also draws the surface, and `.elastic(squash:bounce:haptics:style:)` tunes both.
- Drawn surfaces are liquid glass. `Style` presets come from the house palette: `.standard` (motion only, your label keeps its look), `.signal` (signal-tinted glass) for the one primary action, `.block(.lilac)` and the other blocks for glass tinted with that block and dark ink, `.raised` (clear glass, ink that follows the appearance) for quiet ones. Pass `fill: .clear` for clear glass of your own. `radius`, `height` (never below 44) and `padding` are adjustable.
- States: at rest a drawn surface floats on a soft lift; pressed it squashes toward the finger, the glass darkens and the lift flattens; after a 350ms hold it presses 0.03 deeper and darkens further with a soft impact; a drag past 44pt cancels the press with a soft impact; disabled surfaces turn to clear glass with muted ink instead of fading. The glass doesn't swell under the press on its own: the squash is the press.
- The touch point comes from a simultaneous zero-distance drag. The squash leans 60% of the way toward the finger and keeps that lean through the release, so the button re-expands from where it was pressed. Dragging stretches the shape along the pull and thins it across, keeping its area, with the same rubber-band resistance a scroll view has at its edge; the pull tracks the finger directly, never through an animation.
- The release springs back past rest with more give the further the press was pulled: a tap gets half of `bounce`, a press pulled to the edge all of it. With the default 0.2, a tap lands on the house elastic spring (passing rest by about 8% of the squash) and a full pull on the expressive one (about 15%), so the button is never firmer than a plain chip's release. Dragging past 44pt cancels the press, and it lets go without overshoot, so tearing off reads differently from a tap.
- Reduce Motion removes the squash, lean, stretch and offset, and every release settles without overshoot. A label with no surface dims while pressed instead; drawn surfaces keep their darken and the lift flattening. Reduce Transparency draws the glass solid. Disabled buttons play no haptics.
