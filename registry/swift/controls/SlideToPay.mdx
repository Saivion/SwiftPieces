## Notes

- Drag the thumb to the end to confirm. A soft house aura gathers under your thumb and follows it, growing brighter the further you go.
- Let go before the end and the thumb springs back with the speed you released it at, and the light drains back with it. A quick flick near the end counts.
- At the end the light floods the whole track, the track folds into a red check and a soft ring of light lets go, with a success haptic. `action` runs once.
- `isComplete` stays `true` while it is folded. Set it back to `false` to unfold the track, for example if the payment fails.
- It fills the width you give it and is 60pt tall, with a 48pt thumb.
- VoiceOver reads it as a button and confirms with a double tap.
- Under Reduce Motion the light holds still and still follows the thumb, and there is no ring. Under Reduce Transparency the aura becomes a plain red edge.
