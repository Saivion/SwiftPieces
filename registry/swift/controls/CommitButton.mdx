## Notes

- `phase` is a `Binding` so the button can return itself to `.idle` after the success hold (`successHold`, default 900ms); everything else is set by you: `.loading` in the action, `.success` or `.error("message")` when the work finishes.
- The button is liquid glass. Idle, it is a signal-tinted glass capsule with dark ink; pressed it sinks and the glass darkens a touch. Loading draws the capsule in around its label and buds a round glass bubble out of its end, joined to it by a liquid neck, carrying a signal arc that winds up from rest. Success lets the arc coast to rest as it closes into a ring while the capsule and the bubble turn sage, draws the check and pops it on the success haptic; then, if you pass `successTitle` ("Saved"), the bubble melts back into the capsule's end as the capsule opens to full width and the label morphs letter by letter into the title, a check beside it. Without a title the check stays in its bubble until the hold ends. An error opens the capsule back out on butter glass, morphs the label into the message, turns the bubble into a butter retry and shakes with the error haptic on the same beat. Disabled is clear glass with muted ink.
- A new `title` or `successTitle` reaches the label as soon as it changes, morphing letter by letter, so a checkout's "Pay $64.00" can follow its total. It never restarts the work, the outcome or the hold, and an error keeps showing its message until the next try.
- The label gets the capsule's width and no more: the room you give the button, less the bubble while it is out. A label too long for one line there (a long error, an accessibility text size, a narrow screen) wraps onto a second line, shrinking a little if it must, and the capsule grows taller to hold it instead of wider. It never runs past the screen's edge, with `collapses` on or off.
- Tapping in the error state, on the label or on the retry bubble, fires the action again (VoiceOver hears "Double tap to try again"). The button ignores taps while loading or succeeding without dimming.
- `collapses: false` keeps the capsule full width while it works and makes room for the bubble at its end instead of drawing in around the label.
- `tint` and `errorTint` still work: they override the style's glass tints and switch that state's ink to white. For full control pass a `Style` with your own tints, ink, height and font. `disabledFill` no longer does anything: a disabled button is clear glass.
- Motion uses the shared SwiftPieces springs: the capsule's width, tint and label change together and land with a small give, the bubble leaves on the bud's split spring and goes home with no bounce, its tint draining as it melts in, and only the mark pops. Under Reduce Motion the press darkens instead of sinking, the bubble fades in and out in place, the arc holds still and breathes in opacity while it works, labels cross-fade, and there is no pop or shake; colors, the check, the message and every haptic stay. Reduce Transparency draws the glass solid.
- Need a slide-to-act confirmation with an amount and recipient? SwiftPieces Pro has the Send Money screen (`swipe-to-confirm`).
