## Notes

- Use it like `Toggle`. With a title, the whole row toggles on tap, the way a settings row does. Without one, give the switch an `accessibilityLabel`.
- The thumb has two edges, each on its own spring. The edge facing the move leads on a tight spring and the far edge follows on a looser one, so the thumb stretches across the track and gathers in. The red fill floods out of the thumb as it crosses, so the on state never just appears.
- Under a finger the thumb swells toward the middle. A drag follows the finger with a soft edge at either end and flips at the midpoint, and the release carries the finger's speed into the landing. A touch the system takes over, such as a scroll, puts the thumb back.
- Setting `isOn` from outside moves the thumb the same way a tap does. `symbol` fades an SF Symbol into the thumb as it turns on.
- One light impact haptic fires for each flip. VoiceOver reads the switch as a toggle with its title and an On or Off value, and a double-tap flips it. `.disabled(true)` dims it.
- Under Reduce Motion both edges share one short spring, so the thumb slides whole with no stretch. Reduce Transparency draws the track as a solid shape.
