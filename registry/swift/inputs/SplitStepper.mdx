## Notes

- The share each person pays is the hero. It rolls to each new figure, counting up or down with the direction you stepped.
- Each tap pulses the house aura at the side you pressed, then lets it settle back around the stepper. A dot pops in or out for each person, in eight colours that repeat.
- A tap past `range` shakes the stepper and plays an error haptic. The share stays where it was.
- The share is `total` divided evenly and rounded to cents. Any remainder is left for you to settle, such as on the first person.
- Amounts are shown in `currencyCode` in the person's locale.
- VoiceOver reads the people and the share as one adjustable element: swipe up or down to change it.
- Under Reduce Motion the light holds still and the dots appear without scaling. Under Reduce Transparency the aura becomes a plain red edge.
