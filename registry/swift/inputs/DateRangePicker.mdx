## Notes

- The selection is a `ClosedRange<Date>` of start-of-day dates in the environment `Calendar` and `TimeZone`. `upperBound` is the start of the last day (the check-out day for a stay). Dates you pass in are normalized to their day.
- First tap sets the start and clears any old range, second tap sets the end, third tap starts a new range. Tapping a day before the pending start moves the start there. In `.nights` mode tapping the start again cancels it; in `.days` mode it confirms a one-day range.
- A range never contains an unavailable day. An end tap that would span a day marked by `isDateDisabled` or outside `bounds`, or that exceeds `maximumLength`, is refused: the start stays, an `.error` haptic plays, and a notice chip ("Includes unavailable dates", "Up to 14 nights") replaces the length chip for two seconds. While a start is pending, days that cannot close the range are dimmed.
- The grid comes from the calendar: `firstWeekday`, days per week, days in the month and the weekday offset all come from `Calendar` APIs, so Hebrew, Islamic, Ethiopic and other calendars lay out correctly. Weekday symbols are the calendar's very short standalone symbols; day numbers, month titles and the summary use `Date.FormatStyle` and `Date.IntervalFormatStyle` in the environment locale, including native digits.
- Day math uses `Calendar.date(byAdding:)` and counts days on civil dates, never 86,400 second steps, so 23 and 25 hour DST days and time zones whose midnight is skipped stay exact.
- The band is drawn per cell as half-cell pieces under the endpoint blocks, so it reads as one strip that wraps across week rows with rounded caps at row edges and at month edges. When a range completes it sweeps in from the start, one day at a time. `UnevenRoundedRectangle` uses leading and trailing radii, so caps mirror in right-to-left layouts, where the grid also flows right to left.
- Months page with the chevrons or a horizontal swipe (a paging `ScrollView`), and the title rolls in the paging direction. Paging stops at the months of `bounds`; without bounds it spans five years either side of today and the selection's month.
- The summary shows the range in the locale's interval pattern ("Mar 4 – 9") and a length chip in `counting` units, pluralized with automatic grammar agreement.
- Haptics: `.selection` when a start is set, `.success` when a range completes, `.error` on a refused end.
- VoiceOver: each day is a button labeled with its full date ("Tuesday, March 4", plus the year outside the current one, and "Today" first for today), with the `.isSelected` trait inside the range and a value of Start date, End date, In range or Unavailable. Month changes, completed ranges and refusals are announced.
- Dynamic Type: day cells grow with `@ScaledMetric` from 44pt up to 76pt tall; day numbers scale to accessibility sizes and shrink to fit rather than clip. The summary stacks when it no longer fits on one line.
- Reduce Motion: no sweep, springs or rolling title. The swipe no longer slides the grid; both the swipe and the chevrons crossfade to the next month.
- `Style` sets the ground, day text, muted text, band, endpoint blocks, ink on blocks, the today dot, disabled days, the month buttons, the notice chip and the corner radius. `.disabled(true)` dims the picker and stops taps and paging.
- For booking a time slot inside a day, see the slot picker in Swift Pieces Pro.
