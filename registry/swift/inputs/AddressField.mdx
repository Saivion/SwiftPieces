## Notes

- Typing searches once the trimmed text is three characters or more, after a 250ms pause. The search runs in a task keyed on the text, so every keystroke cancels the one in flight: a slow answer for an older query can never replace a newer list. Earlier suggestions stay on screen while the next ones load, with a small spinner in the field instead of a flicker.
- Suggestions come from `MKLocalSearchCompleter` with `resultTypes = .address`, at most five, the matched runs in bold and the locality on a second line. Picking one runs an `MKLocalSearch` for that completion, with a spinner on the chosen row and the other rows dimmed. Picking another row, editing the text or leaving the screen cancels the lookup, and a late answer is ignored.
- Once resolved, `address` holds the street, city, state, postal code, country, ISO country code, coordinate and a single line formatted for the address's own country. `text` becomes the first line (usually the street), the rest shows under it in the field and a sage check appears. `mailingLines` gives the label lines in the country's postal order.
- Editing the text clears `address`. Setting `address` from outside (a saved address) fills the field without searching; setting `text` and `address` together is not treated as an edit.
- A failed lookup stays on its row in red with a Retry pill; a failed suggestion request shows "Couldn't load suggestions" with Retry. No matches is a quiet line. In each of these cases a "Use as typed" row sets an address with `isVerified == false` holding only the typed text. Turn it off with `allowsUnverified: false`.
- `countries: ["US", "CA"]` leaves out suggestions whose last line names another country, and a picked address outside the list shows "Choose an address in United States" on its row instead of being set. `region` biases suggestions toward an area. Location permission is never requested.
- iOS 26 moves map items to `MKAddress`, which has no separate street or postal code, and deprecates `MKPlacemark`. On iOS 17 to 25 the parts come from the placemark; on iOS 26 from the item's placemark read dynamically (so the file builds without deprecation warnings), with `MKAddressRepresentations` for the formatted line, city and country as the fallback.
- `source:` swaps MapKit for your own backend, or a fixed list for previews and tests. Both calls are cancelled when their result is no longer wanted.
- Hardware keyboard: up and down arrows move the highlight, Return picks the highlighted row (or the top one), Escape closes the list. A long street wraps up to three lines. A pasted multi-line address is joined with commas.
- The list sits inline under the field and pushes content down, so it scrolls with a form and never clips inside a scroll view. Leaving the field hides it; coming back shows it again.
- VoiceOver: the field's hint says whether the address is verified; the suggestion count ("5 suggestions", inflected), "Finding address", the result and any error are announced; rows are buttons read as title and locality with the selected trait when highlighted.
- Haptics: a soft impact on pick, success when the address resolves, error when a lookup fails, selection ticks for arrow-key moves. Nothing per keystroke.
- Dynamic Type: rows and the field grow and wrap; at accessibility sizes the leading glyphs step aside so titles get the width.
- `Style` sets the field, label, secondary label, focus ring, row highlight, success, error and ink colors, the corner radius and the minimum height.
- Reduce Motion: the list, check and rows fade instead of sliding and scaling.
