## Notes

- One `DragGesture(minimumDistance: 0)` covers the whole capsule and decides by start location: the round buttons at each end press, the numeral block in the middle starts a scrub. A press that moves more than 14pt sideways becomes a scrub; one that leaves the capsule by more than 20pt cancels without stepping.
- Pressed buttons invert (glyph color fills the circle) and sink to 0.86 on a quick spring with no overshoot, then spring back with a little give when you let go; a button at its bound dims. A tap bounces its glyph; a hold or a scrub steps too fast for a bounce to read, so they don't.
- Hold-to-repeat starts after 400ms and accelerates from 100ms to 60ms after 1.5s and 30ms after 3s. It runs in a `Task` that is cancelled on release, on cancel (including a touch the system takes over), on disappear, and when a bound is hit.
- Scrubbing consumes one step per 14pt of travel, multiplied by a velocity gain of up to 4x. The value stops when your finger does; nothing coasts. While scrubbing, the block lifts (slightly larger, on a soft shadow) and a ruler fades in a beat later and runs with the finger, faster on a flick. The lift is visual only, so the capsule's layout never changes under the finger.
- Digits roll on a tap, a VoiceOver step or a change from your code, landing without overshoot. While a hold repeats or a scrub steps they change in place, so they stay readable.
- Every step ticks a `.selection` haptic, throttled to one per 35ms during fast repeats and scrubs. A scrub step that lands on the bound and hits the stop in the same moment fires only the stop's impact.
- At a bound, a tap, a repeat tick or a VoiceOver step fires a rigid impact and knocks the block 4pt toward that end and back. A scrub that keeps pushing pulls the block against that end with rubber-band resistance, about 4pt at most, while the ruler stops and the value stays at the bound; the impact fires once each time it reaches the stop, and on release the block springs back at the speed it was moving. The stop holds back no more than 14pt of travel however far you push, so coming back the block relaxes within those 14pt and the value steps one step's travel later.
- `Style` sets the trough, buttons, glyph, the numeral block and its ink, and the height (44pt minimum, 52pt by default). `.standard` uses the butter block; pass `.init(block:)` for another.
- Supply an `.accessibilityLabel`; the value and adjustable action are provided.
- Reduce Motion: no press scale, no lift scale, no glyph bounce and no block movement at a bound. The buttons still invert, the lifted block keeps its shadow, the ruler still runs, digits cross-fade instead of rolling, a bound darkens the block for a beat instead of moving it, and every haptic stays.
