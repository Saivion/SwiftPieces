## Notes

- The picker takes the size it is given, at least 320 pt tall: full screen, in a sheet, or `.frame(height: 340).clipShape(.rect(cornerRadius: 26))` inside a form. Bind `selection`, and pass `onConfirm` for the Confirm button. Add `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` to your Info.plist for the locate button. Full screen, the map already runs under the status bar and the home indicator while the card and the locate button stay clear of them, so leave out `.ignoresSafeArea()`, which would push both under the system bars.
- The pin stays in the middle of the map the card leaves visible. While the map moves it lifts 12 pt, its shadow spreads and a small mark stays on the exact spot; when the map settles it drops with a small bounce and a light tap. Only a real change of the spot under the pin lifts it, so the camera reports MapKit sends on appear, on rotation and on resize do nothing.
- `selection` is set when the pin settles (coordinate only) and again when the address arrives. Its coordinate is always where the pin points; the address is the nearest one found. Setting `selection` from outside, a search result or a saved place, moves the map there, and a place that already has an address is shown without a lookup.
- The address is looked up 300 ms after the map settles. A new settle, or leaving the screen, cancels the lookup in flight and its request, and an answer for a spot that is no longer under the pin is dropped. Answers are cached per picker by position rounded to about 5 m (the 40 most recent), so nudging back to a known spot sends nothing, which also keeps clear of the geocoder's rate limit.
- iOS 26 uses `MKReverseGeocodingRequest`, reading the street and postal code from the map item's placemark (dynamically, as Address Field does) with `MKAddressRepresentations` as the fallback. iOS 17 to 25 uses `CLGeocoder`, kept in a deprecated function that only runs below iOS 26, so the file builds without warnings on both.
- The card has one state per moment: the last address dimmed with "Locating…" while the map moves, loading lines while the address is on its way, street and locality once found, "Dropped pin" with the coordinates where there is no address (open water), and "Couldn't find the address" with the coordinates and Retry when the lookup fails (offline, throttled). Every state keeps the same two lines, and the map's center only moves when the card grows, so nothing jumps as the text changes.
- Confirm is a red capsule. While the map moves its label dims and it is disabled. Tapped while the address is on its way, it spins and waits for it (up to four seconds), then hands over the place; after a failed lookup it hands over the coordinate alone. Return confirms with a hardware keyboard. Leave out `onConfirm` to hide it and read `selection` yourself.
- The locate button asks for When In Use permission on its first tap, never on appear. It spins while finding you, flies the camera to you, and fills in once the pin sits on you; a fix from the last minute is used at once. With approximate location the map shows the area the fix covers instead of a street. When access is denied the arrow is slashed and a tap explains and offers Settings; when it is restricted a tap explains. Dragging works the same without permission.
- Where the map starts: the place in `selection`, else `initialCenter`, else the person's location when the app already has permission, else central Lisbon.
- MapKit's logo and legal notice stay visible above the card: the card insets the map's safe area, which is also where MapKit puts the camera's center.
- VoiceOver: the address readout is one element, "Selected location", with the address, "Dropped pin" and the coordinates, "Locating" or "Finding the address" as its value. Its actions move the map a quarter of the visible span north, south, east or west, zoom in and out, use your location and retry. After the map settles from a move the new address is announced. The pin is decorative and hidden; the locate button reads its state.
- Haptics: a light tap when the pin drops, success when Confirm hands over the place, a selection tick on Retry, and an error when Retry or locating fails. Nothing while panning, and nothing for a lookup that fails on its own.
- Dynamic Type: the card's text grows and wraps up to accessibility sizes, Retry moves under the text, Confirm stays full width, and the locate button grows up to 64 pt. Right to left, the card mirrors and the locate button sits top left; the map is never mirrored.
- Reduce Motion: the pin dims instead of lifting, the camera jumps instead of flying, the loading lines hold still, and buttons dim instead of dipping when pressed.
- The map pans and zooms but doesn't rotate or tilt, so north stays up. A drag that starts on the map pans it, inside a `ScrollView` or a `Form` too; in a sheet, the grabber still dismisses.
- `Style` sets the card surface, label, secondary label, field, the pin and its ink, Confirm and its ink, the card's corner radius, points of interest (off by default, so the streets and the pin read first) and the muted map (on by default). `Messages` replaces any line, for example `confirm: "Deliver here"`. `Geocoder` swaps MapKit for your own backend, or a fixed answer for previews and tests.
