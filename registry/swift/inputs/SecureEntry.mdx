## Notes

- The field is liquid glass, with a round glass reveal bubble joined to its end by a liquid neck. Revealing swaps `SecureField` for `TextField` instantly; the toggle re-targets `@FocusState` to the new field so the keyboard stays up, and its eye glyph blurs into the other. The bubble sinks under the finger.
- Focus draws a 2pt ring around the field. The field's glass carries its state: `Style.error` (butter) while there is an error, `Style.success` (sage) once every requirement passes, neutral otherwise. Text on a tinted field turns to dark ink.
- Everything below the field lines up with it and buds straight down out of it when it appears, then melts back into it when it goes, while the room it takes opens and closes on the same springs, so whatever sits below moves with it instead of jumping.
- Setting `error` from `nil` shakes the field once with an `.error` haptic on the same beat, tints it butter, and buds a butter error chip out of its bottom edge to rest joined just under it. A new message for an error already showing blurs into the new wording in place, with no second shake or haptic, so live validation never buzzes per keystroke. To signal a later refusal, set `error` to `nil` when the user edits, so the next refused submit arrives from `nil` and shakes again. Clearing it melts the chip back into the field as the tint drains.
- The strength chip buds out of the field as typing starts and rests apart below it, beside a gauge that fades in. Its label morphs letter by letter from WEAK through FAIR and GOOD to STRONG, and the chip and gauge take the level's tint: butter while weak or fair, sage once good. The gauge changes length without overshoot, so the reading never wobbles.
- Requirement chips bud out of the field while it is focused or filled, two to a row, resting apart. A met requirement clicks into sage glass with a small dip and swell, its check drawing in after the tint lands, with a `.selection` haptic. When all four pass, the field turns sage and a check blurs into it with a `.success` haptic in place of the tick and the pop of the last chip, and the chips stay a beat so the last one lands, then melt back into the field. Leaving the field melts them at once, and losing a rule during that beat keeps them.
- `Style` sets every tint plus the field height (also the reveal bubble's diameter) and radius. All text is semibold. `.disabled(true)` dims the field.
- Reduce Motion: no shake and no press scale (the reveal bubble shades instead), the chips open and close where they rest instead of budding, the morphing label and the swapped glyphs crossfade, and met chips change tint without the pop. The tints, the drawn checks and every haptic remain. Reduce Transparency draws the glass as solid shapes.
- For one-time codes after sign-in, see the Verify screen (code entry) in SwiftPieces Pro.
