## Notes

- The tile is a liquid glass panel: neutral glass by default, tinted with the block's color for `.block(_:)`. The value is an `Animatable` text in semibold 40 pt display type with dimmed decimals. A spring with no bounce interpolates real numbers through your `format`, so a roll from rest never shows a value past the one it is heading to, and in-flight frames round to the target's own decimal places, so whole-number formats never flash a long fraction mid-roll.
- On first appearance the value counts up as the line draws on, and the dot drops onto the tip once the line has reached it.
- The tile is a weighted card: it dips about 2.5 pt per edge toward your thumb and its shadow flattens. Held for 280 ms anywhere on a tile with a line, it keeps sinking a point deeper while the hold arms, then springs back up as the dot catches the line at the point nearest your finger with a pop and a soft tap. While you scrub, a rule and the lifted dot sit on the nearest point at every frame, the value retargets with a short roll, and `.selection` ticks at each point after the first. A glass flag buds out of the panel's top edge right above the point, out of the finger's way, and rests a neck's width above it, joined by a liquid neck: it shows the change from that point to the latest one, tinted sage for a rise and red for a fall, and rides along the edge with the finger without easing after it. Release rolls back to the live value, the flag's tint drains as it melts back into the panel, and the dot grows back on the latest point with visible give. The flag reaches about 32 pt above the tile, over whatever sits there, for as long as you scrub.
- A plain tap opens the tile like a drawer: the same line grows into a 120 pt chart, with its dot and a dashed baseline at the first value riding the new scale, the baseline fading in a beat behind. The card's edge uncovers a low and high row as it opens, and closing is a little quicker. Tiles around it make room on the same spring. Moving more than 10 pt before the hold completes cancels both, so the tile can live in a scrolling grid, and a touch the system cancels lets go without expanding.
- The delta is a tinted chip with ink on the panel: sage for up, red for down. It is a fill rather than a glass shape of its own, so the tile reads as one surface. It dims while you scrub, while the flag speaks for the point. Under Increase Contrast it gains a 1 pt outline.
- `Style`: `.standard` is a neutral glass tile (its `background` is `.clear`); `.block(_:)` tints the glass with a color block and turns the value, line, and dot to ink, with the delta and a moving flag as ink pills. `tint` overrides the sparkline color. Every string and symbol is semibold; size sets the hierarchy.
- Under Reduce Motion the press dims the tile and flattens its shadow without scaling or sinking, the value and line appear without the count-up and draw-on, each roll shortens to a quarter second, the dot and rule fade instead of scaling, the flag fades in and out in place above the tile instead of budding, and the drawer opens and closes in about 0.2 s with fades. Under Reduce Transparency the glass draws solid. Haptics are unchanged. VoiceOver reads label and value, double-tap expands, and swipe up or down scrubs history; swiping up past the latest point returns to the live value, which then follows new values again.
- A full dashboard screen ships in SwiftPieces Pro (`dashboard-screen`).
