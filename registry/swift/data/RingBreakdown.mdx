## Notes

- Slices are solid color blocks from the house palette (red, sky, butter, sage, lilac, sand), separated by a 4 pt gap with rounded ends. Each slice is filled and stroked with a round join, so the corners stay soft at any size.
- Drag anywhere on the ring and the selection follows your finger around it, with a `.selection` tick at every slice boundary. Release keeps the selection; tap the hole or the selected legend chip to clear it. Only changes made on the ring, the legend or with VoiceOver tick: a parent setting `selection`, or new data clearing it, stays silent.
- The selected slice pulls 6 pt outward along its mid-angle with visible give and lifts a soft shadow, and drops back firmly when it lets go. The others step back to a solid resting color a beat later on a calmer spring, never a faded wash, so a selection reads as one wedge pulling out; clearing brings everything back together. The center is liquid glass over a semibold figure with dimmed decimals: a glass pill names the selection ("Total" when nothing is picked), morphing letter by letter, and the selected slice's share buds out of the pill's end as a chip tinted with the slice's color, resting joined to the pill by a liquid neck while the pair slides over to stay centered. Moving to another slice keeps the chip out: its share rolls and its tint changes in place. Clearing the selection drains its tint and melts it back into the pill. The figure and the share roll without overshoot. The legend is a stack of glass chips resting apart; the picked chip takes its slice's tint with ink text on the slice's beat, the other chips' text steps back on the same follow-through while their glass stays whole, and chips sink slightly when pressed.
- The ring sweeps in clockwise from the top on first appearance, and again only when data lands in an empty ring, while the center pill and figure are there from the start. New values after that slide the seams to their new angles with no overshoot, even when the selection changes with them, the center figure rolls to its new value, and the selection stays while its label is still there. A slice that arrives opens from the seam between its neighbors as they make room, and one that leaves folds back into it, so the ring never shows more than the whole.
- `tint` overrides the first slice; `color` on a slice overrides any slice. `Style` sets `blocks`, `rest`, `ink`, `text`, `muted`, `gap`, and `cornerRadius`. The default thickness is 34 pt. Every string is semibold; size sets the hierarchy.
- The ring is one VoiceOver element: it reads the selected slice or the total, and swiping up or down moves the selection. Legend chips are 44 pt buttons with their value. Reduce Motion drops the lift, the share chip's bud (it fades in and out in place beside the pill), the letter morph (the name crossfades) and the press scale (a pressed chip dims instead), fades the ring in instead of sweeping it, and crossfades digits instead of rolling them. New values still slide the seams, and slices still open and fold, on a shorter spring with no overshoot. Colors, the shadow and the haptics stay. Under Reduce Transparency the glass draws solid.
- Labels are the identity, so keep them unique. `selection` lets a parent drive or observe the selected slice, and a selection set before the ring appears is kept. An id that names no slice draws as no selection.
- A full budget screen built on a spending ring ships in SwiftPieces Pro (`spending-ring`).
