## Notes

- An avatar above a grid of your photos. Tap one and it lifts, arcs `style.arc` points up to the avatar and rounds from a square into a circle as it lands, with a soft bump, a ring and a medium haptic.
- `selection` is the chosen photo's id, so you keep the choice in your own state. Picking another photo flies it in over the current one.
- Pass `name` and `caption` to show a line under the avatar, such as the person's name and "Looking good" once a photo is chosen.
- It takes the width it is offered and grows to the height the grid needs. The chosen photo gets a red outline in the grid.
- Under Reduce Motion the photo travels straight, without the arc, and the avatar neither bumps nor rings.
