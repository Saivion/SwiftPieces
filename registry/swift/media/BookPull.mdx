## Notes

- Draws your own spine and cover views: the shelf lays out each book's `spineSize`, centred, and the tapped book slides up, then turns a quarter turn so its spine narrows as its cover widens to `coverSize`, landing in the middle above the shelf.
- The rest of the shelf dims behind it and your `actions` fade in under the cover. Tap anywhere to put the book back: it turns back and slots into its gap.
- It fills the space it is given, with the shelf at `style.shelf` of the height. Keep the row of spines narrower than the space.
- `selection` is a binding, so you can pull out a book from code too.
- Under Reduce Motion the pull runs as one short, even move.
