## Notes

- `split` is how much of `before` shows, from 0 (only after) to 1 (only before). Give the compare a frame: it fills the size you offer. `before` and `after` are any views, masked by the seam, so you can pass photos, colors, or your own drawings.
- Drag the seam. It tracks the finger and rubber-bands past either edge. A quiet release near the middle (within about 6 percent, and not a flick) settles on the halfway detent and ticks. A harder flick keeps its speed and lands where it was heading.
- The handle is a glass capsule. While the finger is down, a percent bubble buds off it on a liquid neck and melts home on release. The Before and After captions are their own glass chips, not in the handle's group, so they stay separate. On iOS 26 the handle is interactive Liquid Glass. On iOS 17, and under Reduce Transparency, it is a frosted material or a solid fill.
- Haptics: a selection tick when the seam crosses the middle. Pass `haptics: false` to stay silent.
- VoiceOver reads one adjustable control, "Before, 62 percent", and increment or decrement moves the seam.
- Reduce Motion keeps the seam, the detent, and the tick. The percent bubble still appears and disappears, without the travel.
- Right to left flips the drag and which picture leads.
- Captions default to Before and After. Pass `beforeCaption` and `afterCaption` to name the pictures.
