## Notes

- One liquid glass control beside your field. At rest a glass mic rests apart from your content at the trailing end. Recording, the mic swells red and a glass capsule buds out of it over your content, joined to it by a short neck, while a narrow lock lane buds up out of it above. Hands-free, a trash bubble buds out of the capsule's leading end and the mic becomes a red send button. Red appears only on the recording mic, the send button, the recording dot and the last-ten-seconds countdown. On iOS 26 this is Liquid Glass; before that, a frosted material; under Reduce Transparency, solid fills.
- Put it where your composer is, usually in `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)` under a chat `ScrollView`, and pass your field as `content`. Your content is shown as it is (style it yourself) and fills the leading space; it fades out under the capsule while recording and comes back as the capsule melts home. Without `content` the mic rests alone at the trailing end and the capsule spans the whole width.
- Add `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` to your Info.plist (the piece's metadata carries "Records voice messages you send."). The first press asks for the microphone and records nothing; when access is granted the hint "Hold to record, release to send" buds out, and when it is off the hint reads "Microphone access is off" with Settings, which opens your app's settings. Without the usage description it never asks (asking would end the app): it shows the access hint and, in debug builds, prints a reminder.
- Press and hold: after 0.18 seconds with the finger still, recording starts with a medium tap. A quicker tap, or a hold shorter than `minimumDuration` (0.6 seconds), is discarded and teaches the gesture with the hint. A finger that moves first is a scroll: the press gives way and the enclosing scroll view or sheet moves as usual. Once recording, nothing steals the drag.
- Let go to send. The capsule melts back into the mic with a light tap and `onSend` gets a `Recording`: `url`, an AAC `.m4a` (24 kHz, mono) in the temporary directory that is now yours to upload, move or delete; `duration` in seconds; and `samples`, loudness from 0 to 1 at about 20 a second (at most 160, the peak of each stretch), ready for `WaveformScrub(samples:duration:…)`.
- Slide toward the leading edge to discard. The mic follows the finger, the capsule shortens with it, "Slide to cancel" is eaten from its far end and fades, and the lock lane melts away. At the threshold (110 points, less on narrow screens) a glass bin buds out at the leading end with a selection tick; let go there, or keep going well past it, and the capsule gathers into the bin, which tips closed and melts away with a warning tap. The file is deleted and `onCancel` runs. Back short of the threshold, the bin melts home and everything returns.
- Slide up to lock. The lane stretches with the finger; 56 points up the lock shuts with a rigid tick, the lane melts into the mic, the trash bubble buds out of the capsule and the mic turns into the red send button. Tap send to finish, tap trash to discard. A hold on the send button sends when it lifts.
- The capsule shows a red dot that breathes once a second, the elapsed time (`0:07`, `1:02:07` when takes can run an hour; monospaced, so nothing shifts) and live bars, one every tenth of a second, scrolling toward the leading edge from beside the mic. Before there is sound the row is quiet dots. Over the last ten seconds before `maximumDuration` (5 minutes) the time counts down on a red block with ink digits, and at the maximum the take is sent, held or locked, with a success tap.
- Interruptions: a call, Siri, an alarm, a lost audio route, reset media services, the app leaving the foreground, the control being disabled or the view going away discard a held take (the hint reads "Recording stopped" and `onCancel` runs) and stop a hands-free one, keeping it: the dot greys, the time and bars freeze, and Send and Delete still work. The audio session is set to play and record for each take and put back to the app's own category afterwards with `.notifyOthersOnDeactivation`, so other apps' audio resumes.
- Recording runs off the main actor. Takes are numbered, so a release during a slow microphone start never leaves a recorder running, and every discarded take's file is deleted. A take is too short by how long it was held, so a slow start never turns a real hold into a hint.
- `VoiceRecorder.Session` is the recorder's state and audio. Keep one in `@State` and pass `session:` to drive it from code: `start()` (hands-free at once), `press()` and `release()` (push-to-talk from your own control), `lock()`, `send()` and `cancel()`. Read `phase` (`.idle`, `.holding`, `.locked`, `.stopped`), `level` (0 to 1, live) and `elapsed`, for example to dim the conversation while recording. One session drives one recorder.
- `Session(source: .simulated)` records no microphone and needs no permission: a deterministic speech-like level (phrases with short pauses) drives the bars, and the sent file is a soft tone shaped by it, so the URL plays and its waveform matches. Use it in SwiftUI previews, on the simulator and for screenshots.
- Haptics: medium when recording starts, a selection tick at the discard threshold (both ways), rigid when it locks, a warning on discard, light on send, soft with a hint, success when the maximum sends it. Never per frame.
- VoiceOver can't hold and drag, so the mic is a button, "Record voice message": a double tap starts a hands-free take at once, "Recording" is announced, and Send and Delete are buttons. The capsule is one element, "Recording", whose value is the elapsed time ("12 seconds", or "9 seconds left" in the countdown); it stays quiet while recording. The mic's actions are Record, and while recording Send and Delete. Escape (the two-finger scrub) deletes, and a two-finger double tap starts or sends. Hints are announced.
- Hardware keyboard: while hands-free, Return sends and Escape deletes.
- Reduce Motion: the mic doesn't swell, the chevrons don't drift, the capsule, lane, bin and hints open and close in place instead of budding, the bin doesn't tip, and digit changes fade. The bars still move: they are information. Pressed buttons dim instead of sinking.
- Dynamic Type: the capsule, mic and glyphs grow with the text, part way and up to 1.4 times, so the bars keep their room; the time stops growing at the largest standard size. At accessibility sizes "Slide to cancel" drops to its chevron, and hints wrap onto more lines. All text is one weight, semibold.
- Right to left mirrors everything: the mic sits on the left, discards slide to the right (the leading edge), the bars scroll right and the chevron points right. Hints wrap in the reading direction.
- `Style` sets the red accent and the ink on it, the label and secondary label colours, the bars' colour, width and spacing, and the control's height (52 points, at least 44). `Messages` replaces any of the copy, such as `slideToCancel: "Slide to delete"` or `holdToRecord`; every default goes through your String Catalog.
- Wire it up: in `onSend`, upload `recording.url` (or move it into your app's storage first), keep `duration` and `samples` with the message, and show it with `WaveformScrub(samples: message.samples, duration: message.duration, …)`. Delete the file once it is uploaded.
