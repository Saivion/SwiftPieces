## Notes

- Put it in the bottom safe area inset of your main screen, `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom) { NowPlaying(…).padding(.horizontal, 12) }`. Its layout size is only the bar, so your scroll content stops above it, and the card opens over the content above the bar, never past the top safe area.
- Playback is yours. The piece never runs a clock: pass your player's `time`, `duration` and `isPlaying`, and act in `onPlayPause`, `onSkip` and `onSeek`. Time updates are eased linearly over the gap between them, so a player that reports once a second still creeps smoothly.
- Collapsed, it is a glass bar (the artwork, the title over the artist, a red progress hairline) with a red play bubble and a forward bubble joined to its end on liquid necks. Tap the bar, or pull it up: the glass stretches after the finger with a rubber band's resistance, and a pull or flick that would carry past 72 points opens it; a shorter one springs back.
- Opening is one continuous morph: forward melts into play, the same glass grows into the card, the artwork flies from its tile to its square, play flies to the middle of the transport and swells, and previous and next bud out of play and rest apart from it. The title and artist blur out as they leave the bar and back in at the card's size, so they never cross the growing artwork. A pull carries the finger's speed into the morph.
- Drag the card down from anywhere but the scrubber and it follows the finger; let go past about a fifth of its height, or flick, and it closes: the controls blur out, previous and next melt home, the card folds into the bar and forward buds back out. Tap the grabber to close it too. `expanded` follows every change, and setting it from your code runs the same choreography.
- The scrubber: touch the track anywhere and the thumb jumps under the finger and swells; the times follow, your player's updates are ignored while the finger is down, and `onSeek` fires once on release. The thumb then holds the new position until your `time` catches up (or a second and a half passes), so it never jumps back. `onSeek: nil` makes it a read-only progress bar.
- Skipping: `.tracks` calls `onSkip(.previous)` or `onSkip(.next)`; the title morphs letter by letter and the artwork blurs into the new one when your title changes. `.seconds(back: 15, forward: 30)` (or `.podcast`) shows `gobackward.15` and `goforward.30` and seeks through `onSeek` instead. Without a handler the skip bubbles don't appear.
- States: a `duration` of `0` is a live stream (no hairline, no scrubber, Live in place of the times); an empty `title` shows "Not playing"; long titles truncate in one line; the default artwork is a sand tile with a note.
- The card's artwork fills its width and the card hugs the height left above the bar: on a short window it shrinks, and in landscape or at the largest text sizes the card switches to a row with a small artwork beside the lines.
- VoiceOver: collapsed, the bar is one button, "Now playing, Slow Mornings, Aster Vale", valued Playing or Paused, with Play or Pause, Next track and Open player actions. Open, focus moves to the title (a header); the scrubber is adjustable in steps of 15 seconds and reads "1 minute, 12 seconds of 3 minutes, 42 seconds"; the transport and the grabber ("Close player") are buttons, and the escape gesture closes. A skip announces the new title and artist.
- Hardware keyboard: Space plays or pauses, Return opens, Escape closes, and the left and right arrows seek 15 seconds while open.
- Reduce Motion: no stretch under the finger, the glass changes shape on a short ease without overshoot, the artwork and lines fade out and back in at their new places instead of flying, and the bubbles fade in place.
- Dynamic Type: the lines, the artwork tile and the mini buttons scale, the bar grows taller and the lines truncate; the card's line heights follow, and its artwork gives way first. Right to left mirrors the bar and the card's lines; the transport, the scrubber and the hairline keep left to right, as media controls do.
- Haptics: a light impact on play and pause, a selection tick on skip and when a finger grabs the scrubber, and a soft impact when the player lands open or closed.
- Red marks three things only: the play bubble, the progress hairline and the played part of the track. Everything else is clear glass and house ink. All text is one weight, semibold.
- `Style` sets the accent and its ink, the label colours, the track, the thumb, the grabber and the placeholder tile. `Messages` replaces any of the copy and the VoiceOver strings, such as `untitled: "Nothing queued"`.
