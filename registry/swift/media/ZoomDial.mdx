## Notes

- Preset `stops` sit in a glass capsule with the chosen one on a red disc that flows: its leading edge reaches the new stop first and the far edge follows, so it stretches and gathers in.
- Press and hold for about a third of a second and the capsule widens into a dial: a ruler with a tick at each tenth and labels at each whole number, under a fixed red needle. Slide left to turn the value up. A selection haptic ticks at every tenth.
- Let go and the capsule folds back. The disc moves to the nearest stop and shows the exact value, such as 1.6×.
- One touch does both: a short tap picks the stop under it, a hold opens the dial. The value is clamped to `range` and kept to hundredths.
- VoiceOver reads it as one adjustable element, "Zoom, 2×"; swipe up or down to step through the stops.
- Under Reduce Motion the disc slides whole and the capsule changes width without overshoot.
