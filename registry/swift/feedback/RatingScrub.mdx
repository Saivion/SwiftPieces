## Notes

- Stars are soft rounded shapes that fill as solid blocks. They are the content, so they stay solid; the chrome around them is liquid glass. The block follows the score through `Style.levels` (the brand red, sand, butter, sage, sky by default), so a low rating reads differently from a high one at a glance. Pass `tint` to use one color for every score.
- One `DragGesture` with zero minimum distance handles tap and scrub. The row works like soft keys: the star under the finger rises on a short spring with no overshoot, and the two stars either side of it rise a little after it, less with distance. Let go and the star you left drops first and each neighbor follows 30ms later, so the row falls back in a ripple. The lift is decoration only: the fill and numeral land on the value with no delay or overshoot, quickly under the finger and on a firm spring from VoiceOver or the binding, so a star never shows a score that isn't true.
- While you scrub, a glass readout tinted with the score buds up out of the touched star: born behind it as a small round bubble, it rises above the lifted star while it widens to fit the rating and its label, then rides from star to star with your finger, changing tint and morphing its word as it goes. Let go and it narrows and sinks back into the star you left, its tint draining as it melts. A quick tap never raises it, so a single rating stays quiet.
- With `labels`, a caption sits under the stars: a glass pill with the numeral, a dimmed decimal and "/ 5", joined by a liquid neck to a glass chip tinted with the score that carries the label for the rounded-up rating. The chip morphs its width, tint and word letter by letter as the rating changes. Unrated, the chip is clear glass reading "Tap or slide".
- Pull past either end of the row and the end star gives a few points with rubber-band resistance, about the gap between two stars, then springs back at the speed you let go. The rating itself stays at 0 or the maximum. A scrub the system cancels, such as a scroll taking over, lets the stars and the readout go the same way.
- Every string is semibold, so size and color carry the hierarchy. Before iOS 26 the readout, pill and chip draw on a frosted material with a light rim, and the pill and chip sit side by side without a neck. Under Reduce Transparency they are solid.
- Reduce Motion: nothing rises, ripples or gives past the ends. The star under the finger shifts a shade in place instead (deeper in light mode, lighter in dark), the readout fades in and out above it at full width, the words crossfade, and the haptics and colors are unchanged.
- Haptics: `.selection` on each step, a rigid impact when the value hits 0 or the maximum.
- `isReadOnly` removes the gesture, the readout and the VoiceOver adjustable action but keeps the value readable; its chip reads "No rating" until there is one. `size` scales with Dynamic Type.
