## Notes

- Three outcomes share one choreography, a stamp landing: a ring is drawn in ink, color pools inward from it once it closes and the ring takes the block's color (sage for `.success`, butter for `.failure`, sky for `.empty`), then the mark is pressed in. The outcome lands on one beat: its haptic, a small thud of the block and one ripple. The headline and message rise together, and the actions follow a beat behind, landing with a little give. Change the view's `.id` to replay it from the start.
- Passing a new `outcome` to the same view restamps in place instead of swapping: the old mark lifts as the block recolors, the new mark is pressed in, and the new outcome lands with its own haptic. The copy and actions crossfade and reflow together, and the action's label morphs letter by letter to its new title. A view that reappears keeps its landed outcome and plays nothing again.
- `.empty` draws an open tray and plays a light impact, for first-run and no-results states.
- The headline is set large and tight at `.largeTitle`; `eyebrow` adds a small uppercase line above it. Every string is semibold, so size and color carry the hierarchy.
- The primary action is a liquid glass capsule tinted with `signal` (the brand red, `#FF0000`, by default) under dark ink. It sinks a few points while pressed and springs back with a little give. `secondary` is a quiet text button.
- On failure, `retry` turns the primary action into "Try again". Tapping it morphs the label to "Retrying" while a round glass bubble with a spinner buds out of the button's trailing end: born inside the end, it springs out on a liquid neck as the button gives up its width, and rests joined to it. When the work returns, the bubble melts back in on a spring with no bounce and the button takes its width back.
- `details` adds a glass panel, apart from the button, that opens to selectable monospaced text and closes a little quicker than it opens.
- `Style` holds the three blocks, the ink, the signal tint and its ink, the text colors and the details panel's glass tint (`raised`, clear by default), with house palette defaults that adapt to light and dark. `accent` still overrides the block.
- Before iOS 26 the button, bubble and panel draw on a frosted material with a light rim, and the bubble sits beside the button without a neck. Under Reduce Transparency they are solid.
- Under Reduce Motion the stamp fades in whole instead of being drawn, then lands with its haptic, and the copy and actions fade in a beat later without rising. A restamp recolors the block and swaps the mark briefly, with no thud or ripple. The label crossfades, the progress bubble fades in and out beside the button, the pressed button shifts a shade instead of sinking (darker in light mode, lighter in dark), and the details fade open.
