## Notes

- Write the headline as a list: string literals are words and pictures go where you put them, for example `["Slow", .art(.sun), "mornings, long", .art(.waves), "walks."]`. A string can hold several words; each word rises on its own.
- Pictures land as stickers: a white rim, a soft shadow and a small resting tilt that alternates along the headline (`Style.tilt`, 4 degrees at most), so a line reads as placed by hand. As each one lands, four hairline crop marks flash at its corners, the crosses Swift Pieces draws on its artboards. `Style.tilt = 0` lays them flat, `Style.rim = 0` drops the rim and `Style.cropMarks = false` skips the marks.
- Press and hold a sticker to lift it: it straightens, scales up and casts a deeper shadow above its neighbours, with a light tick, then springs back to its tilt when you let go. The press uses `onLongPressGesture(onPressingChanged:)` with a small tolerance, so scrolling over the headline still scrolls. `Style.lifts = false` turns it off.
- Pictures are sized from the font: `Style.pictureHeight` is a fraction of the line height (0.82 by default) and each picture's `width` is in picture heights (2.4 is a wide frame, 1 a square). Set the font with `.font(...)` as usual and the pictures follow it, Dynamic Type included.
- Words and pictures wrap like text with a private `Layout`, so the headline breaks across rows at any width and a picture never splits from the row it lands on. `alignment` sets each row's alignment.
- The reveal runs in reading order. Each word slides up from behind its own baseline and each picture opens from a dot as tall as the frame into the full sticker on a spring, turning from flat to its tilt as it lands, its contents settling from a slight zoom. The stagger is sized from the number of items, so a short and a long headline both land in about `duration` (1.2 seconds by default). `delay` holds the start; change `trigger` to replay.
- Three kinds of picture: `.art(_:)` draws one of five built-in scenes in the house palette (`.sun` is the red Swift Pieces mascot, bobbing and blinking over the sand; `.waves`, `.moon`, `.hills` and `.bloom`), so a headline looks finished before you have artwork; `.image(_:)` fills the frame with your own `Image`; `.symbol(_:fill:ink:)` sets an SF Symbol on a solid block.
- After landing, pictures keep moving: scenes drift (the sun bobs, the sea slides, stars twinkle), images pan slowly and symbols bob. Set `Style.drift = false` for still pictures. Drawing uses `Canvas` driven by a `TimelineView` at up to 30 frames a second.
- `revealOnScroll` waits until the headline scrolls into view with `onScrollVisibilityChange` on iOS 18; on iOS 17 it reveals on appear.
- Haptics: a light tick as each picture lands, in time with the spring, and another when you lift one. Pass `haptics: false` to stay silent.
- VoiceOver reads the headline as one header: the words in order, plus a picture's `label` where it sits, so `.art(.sun, label: "a sunrise")` is read and an unlabelled picture is skipped.
- Reduce Motion drops the rise, the zoom, the tilt, the crop marks, the lift and the drift: words and pictures fade in together and pictures sit flat and still.
- `Style` (`.standard` is the house look): `pictureHeight`, `cornerRadius` (a fraction of the picture height; 0.5 makes a capsule), `tilt`, `rim`, `rimColor`, `cropMarks`, `lifts`, `drift`, `openDuration` and `openBounce`.
