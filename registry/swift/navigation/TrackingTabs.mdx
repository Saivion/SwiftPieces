## Notes

- Pages live in a horizontal `ScrollView` with `.scrollTargetBehavior(.paging)` and `containerRelativeFrame`, so each page is exactly one container wide. `selection` is synced with `scrollPosition(id:)` in both directions: a tap, or a change to `selection` from outside, lands the page on a spring with no overshoot, like native paging, so the pager never shows past its last page, and the pager fits the new page's height on that same spring, so both arrive as one move.
- The titles sit on a clear liquid glass track, and the indicator is a signal-tinted glass capsule inside it, in the same liquid. It is positioned from fractional page progress (`contentOffset.x / containerSize.width` via `onScrollGeometryChange` on iOS 18, the page row's position in the `.scrollView` space on iOS 17), interpolated between measured title frames. Under a swipe it is a pure function of the scroll, coupled to the pages like a carriage on a rod: it follows them with no lag of its own, carries a flick's deceleration and lands where they land. While it travels it swells a couple of points, staying inside the track, and it settles flat on a title. A tap that jumps several pages keeps it swollen over the titles it passes, so it only settles where it lands. Coming in from a tap or an outside change it has a little give: it carries a couple of points past its title and snaps back while the page lands flat.
- Each title is drawn twice, muted and ink, each cut by the indicator's outline, so the text turns ink exactly where the glass covers it and stays muted outside it. Optional `counts` show a monospaced count after each title and roll with `.numericText`. Every title and count is semibold.
- States: resting, swiping (indicator between titles, swollen), pressed (the title sinks a couple of points; pressing the selected title sinks the indicator with it), and a `.selection` tick each time `selection` changes, from a tap or a swipe.
- `Style` sets `indicator` (its glass tint), `title`, and `selectedTitle`; `.standard` is a signal indicator with house ink on it and muted titles on the clear track. `track` no longer does anything, since the track is clear glass; it stays so existing code compiles.
- Titles are buttons with selected traits and their count as the value; 44 pt tall. Under Reduce Motion a tap lands the page and the indicator on a short spring with no overshoot, the indicator keeps its size while it travels, and a pressed title shades instead of sinking (darker in light mode, lighter in dark) while the indicator stays put. The indicator and the ink still follow your swipe, and the tick still plays. Before iOS 26 the track and indicator are frosted material with a light rim, and under Reduce Transparency they are solid.
- A floating tab bar with a lens that stretches toward the finger ships in SwiftPieces Pro (`lens-tab-bar`).
