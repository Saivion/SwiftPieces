## Notes

- Put it over a long `ScrollView` with `.overlay(alignment: .bottom)`, and give the island the same binding as the scroll view's `scrollPosition(id:)`: each section's view carries `.id(section.id)` inside a `.scrollTargetLayout()`. Scrolling the page updates the island; a pick, a scrub, an arrow key or the back-to-top bubble sets the binding, and the scroll view takes the page there. A `nil` selection reads as the first section.
- It sits 8 points above the bottom safe area and 16 from each side, rides above the keyboard, and only takes touches on its own glass, so the page behind it keeps scrolling everywhere else.
- Closed, it is one glass capsule hugging its bar: a 26 point ring whose red arc is the part of the page read, the section's title (it morphs letter by letter as the page moves on, and truncates with an ellipsis when it can't fit), a quiet "3 of 6" and a chevron. One section shows its title alone; an empty list draws nothing.
- Pass `progress` from the scroll offset for a true reading (the doc comment and the example show the iOS 18 `onScrollGeometryChange` that computes it). `nil` estimates it from the section's place. The arc tracks a scroll as it goes and sweeps without overshoot after a jump.
- Once the reader is past the first section a round back-to-top bubble buds out of the island's leading end and rests joined to it on a short liquid neck. Tapping it selects the first section; it melts back into the island when the page is back at the top, and while the contents are open. `showsTopButton: false` leaves it out.
- Tap the island, or swipe up on it, and the same glass grows upward in place into the contents (up to `style.maxWidth`, 360 by default, and about 60% of the space it is given). The bar stays where it was, now reading the contents title and how much has been read; the rows arrive once the glass has grown and leave before it shrinks. Longer lists scroll inside the panel, opened on the current section.
- In the contents the current section sits on a red puck with its title in dark ink. Press a row, or slide over the rows (a swipe up that carries on onto a row does the same, like a menu), and the puck follows: its leading edge first and the far edge a beat behind, so it stretches toward each row and gathers on it, with a selection tick per row. Let go and the page jumps there as the panel melts back into the pill. Lifting off the rows, or a touch the system takes, picks nothing. When the rows scroll, a tap picks instead.
- Drag the closed island sideways to step through sections live, one per 32 points, with a tick for each; the title morphs as you go, the island gives with rubber-band resistance past the first and last, and letting go commits. The axis is decided after 8 points, so a swipe up opens it rather than scrubbing. Right to left mirrors the drag: later sections lie toward the trailing side.
- Bind `isExpanded` to open or close the contents from your own controls (a toolbar button, say) or to know when they are open; omit it and the island keeps that state itself. A selection that changes while the contents are open moves the puck.
- VoiceOver: closed, the island is one adjustable button, "Contents", with a value such as "Method, Section 3 of 6, 62% read" and the hint "Shows every section"; swipe up or down to step sections, double-tap to open. Open, focus moves into the list onto the current section; each row is a button with the selected trait and its reading time as the value, the bar closes the contents, and Escape (the two-finger scrub) closes them too. The bubble reads "Back to top".
- Hardware keyboard: focus the island, then the left and right arrows step sections and Return, Space or the up arrow opens the contents; open, the up and down arrows move the puck, Return or Space picks and Escape closes.
- Reduce Motion: the glass grows and shrinks on a short ease, the puck slides on one short spring without stretching, the title cross-fades instead of morphing, the bubble appears and leaves in place, the scrub has no lift or give, and a press shades instead of sinking. Reduce Transparency draws solid surfaces through the shared foundation.
- Dynamic Type: the bar, the ring, the rows and the bubble scale. At the accessibility sizes the closed island drops the quiet position (VoiceOver still reads it) so the title keeps its room, and the contents scroll sooner. All text is one weight, semibold.
- Haptics: a selection tick for each section a scrub or the puck crosses, an arrow key or the bubble; a light impact when the contents open and close.
- Red is the read arc and the puck, nothing else. `Style` sets the accent, the ink on it, the label and secondary label colours, the ring's track and `maxWidth`. `Messages` replaces any of the copy: the contents title (such as "On this page"), the bubble's label, the hints, and the position, spoken position and read amount as closures over formatted numbers.
