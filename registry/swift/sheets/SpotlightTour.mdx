## Notes

- Two modifiers. `.spotlightAnchor("compose")` marks a control as a stop; `.spotlightTour(isPresented:steps:)` goes once on the container that holds them, ideally the `NavigationStack` or `TabView` so the scrim covers the bars too, or a sheet's root. Each `SpotlightTour.Step` names its anchor, a title and a message, and optionally an SF Symbol, the cutout's shape (`.automatic`, `.capsule`, `.circle`, `.rectangle(cornerRadius:)`) and its padding.
- Put the anchor right on the control, before modifiers that widen its frame: `.spotlightAnchor("add")` then `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity, alignment: .trailing)`, not the other way round. Anchors report their frame in window coordinates through the environment, so toolbar items, safe area insets and other hosted content work. Use one id per stop.
- While a stop shows, everything else is dimmed and a 2 pt red ring draws itself around the cutout from the top. Next springs the cutout to the next stop, morphing between rounded rectangles, capsules and circles, and the callout travels with it while its text dips out and back so words never smear. `.automatic` picks a circle for small square targets, a capsule for short wide ones and a rounded rectangle for the rest, with corners running parallel to the target's.
- The callout sits below its target when it fits, else above, else on the roomier side, always inside the safe area and the keyboard with 16 pt margins and at most 360 pt wide. Its nub points at the target's centre and hides when the callout had to overlap the target (very large text on a small screen).
- Taps inside the cutout reach the real control, UIKit bar buttons included. With `advancesOnTargetTap` (the default) the tap also moves the tour on. Taps and drags on the scrim are absorbed with a small nudge of the callout and a light tap, so nothing underneath changes by accident. Swipe the callout toward the end of the line for Next, back toward its start to go back.
- Steps whose anchor isn't on screen (a lazy row scrolled away, a view that isn't there yet) are skipped, and the count shows only the steps on screen. While no anchor is on screen the tour waits without dimming anything, then starts when one appears. The tour gives an anchor a moment before moving on, so toolbar items that report late and the frame changes of a rotation never make it jump. The cutout follows its target through scrolling, rotation, size changes and the keyboard.
- `isPresented` works with `@AppStorage`: `@AppStorage("inbox.showTour") var showTour = true` shows it until it is finished or skipped. Or pass `showOnceKey: "inbox.tour.v1"` and the tour presents itself the first time the view appears and records completion in `UserDefaults`; `.spotlightTour(steps, showOnceKey:)` does the same without a binding. `SpotlightTour.resetShowOnce(_:)` brings it back, for a "Show tips again" setting.
- `step:` takes an optional binding to the current step, to resume a tour or move it on when someone completes the thing you pointed at. The tour writes it as people move through and sets it back to 0 when it ends. `onFinish` reports `.completed` or `.skipped`.
- VoiceOver: while the tour shows it is modal and the screen under it is hidden; focus moves to the callout, which reads "Step 2 of 4. Find it fast. Narrow the inbox by sender, date, attachments or unread." Skip and Next are buttons, Previous step is a custom action, the escape gesture skips, and each step change is announced.
- Hardware keyboard: Return is Next or Done, Escape skips, and the arrow keys move along the steps in reading order. The tour never takes focus, so a text field keeps its keyboard.
- Dynamic Type: the callout grows and flips above its target when it no longer fits below. At accessibility sizes Next and Skip stack full width, the symbol tile steps aside and the callout may use the full width. RTL mirrors the callout; the cutout stays on the physical control.
- Reduce Motion: the cutout and callout crossfade from stop to stop instead of travelling, the ring appears without drawing, and nothing scales.
- Haptics: a selection tick when a step changes, a soft impact when the tour ends, a light tap for a nudge. Nothing when the tour appears on its own.
- All copy goes through `String(localized:)` and can be replaced with `Messages` (Next, Done, Skip, Previous step and the "2 of 4" and "Step 2 of 4" formats). `Style` sets the scrim, the accent used for the ring, the current step and the Next capsule, its ink, the callout surface and text colors, the track, the corner radius, the ring width and the callout's widest size.
