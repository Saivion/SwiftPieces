## Notes

- The sheet previews what the permission brings. Your `benefits` become a fanned stack of notification banners above the title: each is a solid card with its symbol on a tinted app tile (butter, sage and lilac in order by default), its line, and "now". The two behind the front banner sit a little smaller, lower and a shade darker, so their edges read as separate cards. Keep each line short, like a notification ("Design review in 30 minutes"). Without benefits the sheet leads with the icon tile instead.
- Present it in a `.sheet` with `.presentationBackground(PermissionSheet.Style.standard.surface)`, sized to its content (the example measures it for a `.height` detent); it also works inline in any container. The title and message are centred under the stack. About 0.3 s in, once a sheet has settled, the banners drop in from above, the front one first and the rest 90ms apart behind it.
- While the sheet waits for an answer, the stack deals its next banner to the front every 2.6 s on the settle spring, so each benefit gets read. It stops once the request is tapped, and never runs under Reduce Motion. VoiceOver reads the whole stack as one element listing every benefit.
- `request` is your real permission call (for example `UNUserNotificationCenter.requestAuthorization`). The request is a signal glass capsule that sinks under the finger; a tap brings in a spinner and morphs its label letter by letter to Requesting until the call returns. Nothing waits on an animation: the button's action and enabled state change the moment the answer arrives.
- `true` pops the stack and plays `.success`, morphs the button to Allowed with a check on sage glass while the Not now capsule melts up into it (its place keeps its size, so the button never shifts), then calls `onGranted` about a second later so you can dismiss. Without benefits the tile turns sage with a check instead.
- A denial stays calm, with no shake: the stack greys out to half strength, which reads as notifications off, and `.warning` plays. The title and message lift away through a blur and the denied copy rises into their place, and the button morphs to Open Settings on glass the label's color and opens `UIApplication.openSettingsURLString`. Without benefits the tile turns sand with a gear.
- Requesting, Allowed, Open Settings and "now" go through `String(localized:)`.
- `Style` sets the surface, labels, the icon tile's colors, the banners' tile tints (`benefitTiles`, or `[.clear]` for neutral tiles), the action color and the tile radius.
- On iOS 26 the request and Not now are Liquid Glass and melt into each other through the neck; before that they are frosted glass with a light rim, and with Reduce Transparency they are solid.
- Under Reduce Motion nothing drops, blurs, bounces or pops: the banners fade in already stacked and are never dealt, the labels and the two copies crossfade in place, Not now fades where it rests, and the capsules dim while pressed instead of sinking. Haptics are unchanged.
- At accessibility text sizes the button's label is plain text that can wrap, and changes with a blur instead of the letter morph.
- **v3:** redesigned around a preview of the notifications themselves. The benefit rows became the banner stack, and the copy is centred under it. The public API is unchanged.
