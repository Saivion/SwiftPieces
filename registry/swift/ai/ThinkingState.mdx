## Notes

- Every presentation shares the `StreamingReply` layout: a liquid glass header pill (breathing ink mark, name and a THINKING · 3S elapsed label that counts up) and then content sized to a body line, so swapping `ThinkingState` for a `StreamingReply` lands on the same pill and does not shift anything. Set `style.showsHeader = false` for content only.
- `.dots` rises three block-colored dots (tangerine, sky, lilac by default), `.sheen` sweeps a band across reply-shaped bars, and `.text(label)` sweeps the band through the label's glyphs.
- One `TimelineView` drives the mark's breath, the dots and the sheen, so they stay phase-locked. It starts when the placeholder appears: the first pass enters from the leading edge and the dots rise from rest. `duration` sets the period of one pass.
- Each pass eases the band across over the first 80% of `duration`, then rests just clear of the view before the next. The band slides whole, so it keeps its width as it enters and leaves. The mark draws in and turns on the same beat and is back to full size and upright between passes, which is the `StreamingReply` mark's pose. Each dot eases out of rest and back, as in `StreamingReply`.
- Each second the count morphs: the digits it shares with the last one hold still and the new ones blur in, in tabular figures, so the pill only widens (with a little give) when the count gains a digit. THINKING · holds still.
- Pass `tint` to use one color for the dots, mark and sheen.
- `.thinkingState(_:tint:)` masks a bright sweep to the view's own alpha, so it follows capsules, glyphs and text with no clipping setup. It runs the same paced 2.2-second pass, starting from the leading edge each time `isActive` turns true, and fades in and out with `isActive`. On a glass pill, put it on the label inside the glass, as the example does, so the sweep runs through the words and the glass stays clear.
- `Style` (`ThinkingState.Style`): `dots`, `bar`, `sheen`, `mark`, `markInk`, `ink`, `text`, `muted`, `assistantName`, `showsHeader`.
- Every string is semibold. Under Reduce Motion nothing moves, but the placeholder still shows it is working: the band becomes an even glow that brightens and fades in place on the same beat (over the bars, through the label's glyphs, and in the modifier), the dots hold still and ripple their opacity, the mark rests full size and upright, and the elapsed seconds crossfade. Reduce Transparency makes the pill solid.
