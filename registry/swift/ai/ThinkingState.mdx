## Notes

- Every presentation shares the `StreamingReply` layout: a header (breathing ink mark, name and a THINKING · 3S elapsed label that counts up) and then content sized to a body line, so swapping `ThinkingState` for a `StreamingReply` does not shift anything. Set `style.showsHeader = false` for content only.
- `.dots` rises three block-colored dots (tangerine, sky, lilac by default), `.sheen` sweeps a band across reply-shaped bars, and `.text(label)` sweeps the band through the label's glyphs.
- One `TimelineView` drives the mark's breath, the dots and the sheen, so they stay phase-locked. `duration` sets the sweep period.
- Pass `tint` to use one color for the dots, mark and sheen.
- `.thinkingState(_:tint:)` masks a bright sweep to the view's own alpha, so it follows capsules, glyphs and text with no clipping setup, and fades out when `isActive` turns false. It reads best on solid blocks.
- `Style` (`ThinkingState.Style`): `dots`, `bar`, `sheen`, `mark`, `markInk`, `ink`, `text`, `muted`, `assistantName`, `showsHeader`.
- Under Reduce Motion the clock holds still: the band sits mid-view, the dots rest and the mark keeps a steady size; only the elapsed seconds update.
- Need a live timeline of tool calls with durations and results? That is the Tool Execution Card screen in Swift Pieces Pro.
