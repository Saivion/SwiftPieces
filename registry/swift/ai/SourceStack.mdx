## Notes

- Closed, the stack is one pill: the first `stacked` sources (3 by default) as overlapping badges, each a colour disc with the site's initial ringed in the surface so they stay apart, then the count ("4 sources", inflected for one) and a chevron. Place it directly under the answer it supports. `alignment` puts the pill at the leading edge (the default, under an answer) or centred when the stack stands on its own; the list always spans the width.
- Tapping the pill opens the list. The pill's badges fly out of the stack into their own cards on the morph spring with a little give, while the cards cascade in under them 40ms apart, rising 10pt as they fade in. Sources past `stacked` arrive with their cards. The pill drops to the count alone and its chevron turns over. Closing reverses it on the quicker dismiss spring: the cards fade together and the badges gather back into the pill.
- Each card shows its badge, the title (up to two lines), the site and optional `detail` (a date, say), and its citation number, matching the `[1]` markers in your answer text. A tap opens `url` through `openURL`, or calls `onOpen` when you pass one, so you can show the page in-app.
- Pass `isExpanded` to open the list from your own code, for example when someone taps a `[2]` marker in the answer. A change from outside runs the same flight as a tap.
- The pill and every card press in with the house press: a size-aware sink that springs back with a little give. One selection haptic per open or close, one light impact per source opened.
- VoiceOver reads the pill as "4 sources, collapsed" with a hint, and each card as one link: "Source 2: Tram 28, stop by stop, Tram Notes, Updated May". The badges and numbers are decoration and stay out of the tree.
- Reduce Motion: the badges move without overshoot on a short spring, the cards fade in without rising, and presses shade instead of sinking. Haptics are unchanged.
- Dynamic Type: titles and site lines scale freely; badges stop growing at xxxLarge so the stack stays a stack.
- `Style` sets the surface, text, secondary and ink colours, the badge `blocks` (sky, butter, sage, lilac, sand and signal by default) and the card corner radius.
