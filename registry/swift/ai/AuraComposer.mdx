## Notes

- The aura is the `auraComposer` color shader in `AuraComposer.metal`: four colour pools drifting on slow closed paths over a wash of their average, blended by soft weights the way a mesh gradient blends its points, with a little grain so it never bands. It fills a rounded rectangle behind the glass, a little larger than the composer and blurred, so the glass carries its colour and its light spills past the edges. Copy the `.metal` file next to the `.swift` file and the kernel compiles into your app's default library.
- The aura has three moods, each a glow and an energy: at rest a faint still glow (0.35); focused it wakes and drifts (0.7); while `isResponding` it brightens, and its pools leave their paths on a quicker wander, so it churns rather than just speeds up (1). Set the three glows with `Style.glow`. Energy eases between moods, so a change of state never jumps the aura.
- The aura's timeline runs only while the field is focused or the assistant is responding, and for a moment after either changes so the energy can settle. At rest it draws once. Its loop wraps at 2π, where every path closes, so it has no seam however long it runs.
- The field and the send bubble are liquid glass in one `PieceLiquidGroup`, resting `PieceLiquid.joined` apart so a liquid neck holds them as one control. The field grows to six lines and the bubble stays at the bottom edge. The bubble is neutral glass until the trimmed draft has text, then signal red with an ink arrow; while responding it is ink with a stop square, and the symbol swaps with `.symbolEffect(.replace)`.
- Sending hands the trimmed draft to `onSend`, clears the field, pops the bubble and plays a light impact. Stop calls `onStop` with a rigid impact. Drive `isResponding` from your own state: the composer never sets it.
- `onAttach` adds a plus inside the field's leading edge, pressed like a chip.
- Reduce Motion holds the aura still: it keeps its colours and takes each mood's glow at once. Reduce Transparency draws the field and bubble as solid fills, and the aura still glows behind them.
- VoiceOver reads the field by its placeholder, and the bubble as Send or Stop responding. Replace the button labels with `labels:` to localize them.
