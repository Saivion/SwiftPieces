## Notes

- Requires `AssistantOrb.metal` in the same target. The `[[ stitchable ]] assistantOrbSiriWave` function is looked up through `ShaderLibrary` at runtime. Its helpers live in the `assistantOrbSiri` namespace, so it can share a target with other orb kernels.
- The orb has one look: thinking. Four thin sine membranes in blue, pink, orange and red travel left to right through the equator over a violet ground, seen through a refracting glass shell with a cool and warm rim. There is no idle or speaking phase, no audio level and no touch interaction. Colour is the only input: `palette` defaults to `.siri`, and `AssistantOrb.Palette(bands:ground:crest:rimCool:rimWarm:)` takes hex values or `Color`s for your brand. A palette change crossfades all eight colours on a calm spring, so a second change mid-fade turns from the colours on screen; under Reduce Motion it is a short colour fade. The motion never changes.
- The sphere never scales, floats or pulses; only the wave inside it moves. It has no entrance of its own, so it arrives with whatever presents it. Put the orb on a dark surface and pair it with your own status copy if the step changes.
- `size` sets a square frame. The ball fills 90% of it, so leave the frame square.
- The clock restarts each time the orb appears, so the wave always enters on the same first pose and `Float` precision holds over long sessions.
- Under Reduce Motion the wave rests on that first pose: a still frame that nothing moves or dims, and a palette change only recolours it in place. Your status copy carries the sense of progress there. Turning Reduce Motion off lets the wave flow on from that pose without a jump.
- VoiceOver reads "Assistant" with "Thinking" as its value.
