## Notes

- Requires `ThoughtOrb.metal` in the same target. The `[[ stitchable ]] thoughtOrbSiriWave` function is looked up through `ShaderLibrary` at runtime. Its helpers live in the `thoughtOrbSiri` namespace, so it can share a target with other orb kernels.
- The mark has one look: thinking. Four thin sine membranes travel left to right through the equator of a dark glass ball. There are no motion states and no drag; the sphere never scales or pulses, only the wave inside it moves. The wave's clock starts as the mark appears.
- Colour says what kind of work is happening. Presets: `.siri` for thinking (blue, pink, orange, red over violet), `.searching` (electric blue, aqua and mint), `.reading` (indigo, lavender and a teal glint) and `.writing` (red, amber, rose and violet). Build your own with `ThoughtOrb.Palette(bands:ground:crest:rimCool:rimWarm:)`. A change crossfades on a calm spring, so steps that change faster than the fade glide from colour to colour instead of stacking.
- `ThoughtOrb.Pill` is liquid glass: the mark rides its own round glass bubble, joined by a liquid neck to a glass capsule holding the label and the dots. They are two parts of one control, so they rest a neck's width apart and the neck holds. The shader ball sits in its bubble rather than on glass, so it is never glass on glass.
- Drive it from the current step: `ThoughtOrb.Pill(step.title, palette: step.palette)` morphs the label into the new verb letter by letter, the letters both verbs share holding still while the rest blur through, as the capsule's width and the dots at its end reshape around it in the same quick beat, a 0.3s spring with a little give at the end, quick so it stays light in a toolbar. The mark's bubble stays put; only its colour follows on its slower fade, and the motion keeps thinking. `ThoughtOrb(size: 64)` works at avatar size.
- `Pill` derives type, the glass around the mark and the dots from `markSize` (0.47x label, a bubble 1.4x the mark) and scales the whole thing with Dynamic Type through `@ScaledMetric`. Every string is semibold. Each dot rises and falls on an eased curve, then rests, in a ripple from left to right that starts from rest as the dots appear. They run on the clock rather than a repeating animation so they stay in phase however long the pill is up; VoiceOver hears the whole label with "Thinking" as its value and skips the dots. On iOS 26 the glass is Liquid Glass; earlier it is a frosted material with a light rim, and Reduce Transparency makes both shapes solid.
- With an `action`, the pill is a button: its glass and what it carries sink together a touch while pressed, centered so it is safe in toolbars and scrolling content, and it plays one soft tap when the action fires.
- Under Reduce Motion the wave rests on the pose it starts from, a fixed frame that holds through label and colour changes, and the light in it breathes instead: the four bands dim by a quarter and come back over 2.4s, then rest at full for 0.8s, redrawn at 30 frames a second. The glass, rim and crest stay lit and still, so even a bare `ThoughtOrb(size: 64)` reads as thinking with nothing travelling. In the pill the dots keep cycling too, since they only change opacity. The label crossfades instead of morphing, the capsule resizes on a short spring, a palette change is a short fade, and a press shades the pill instead of sinking it (darker in light mode, lighter in dark). Turning Reduce Motion off resumes the wave from its rest pose without a jump.
