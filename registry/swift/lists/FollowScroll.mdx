## Notes

- Opens on the newest item and, while the reader is within `followThreshold` (48pt) of the bottom, scrolls each new item into view with a short smooth scroll. Many items arriving in one update scroll once.
- Once the reader scrolls up, new items never move the content. They are counted into a floating pill at the bottom, such as "3 new", with a red dot and a down arrow. Tapping it (or ⌘↓ on a hardware keyboard) jumps to the latest and clears the count; scrolling back down by hand clears it too. Items removed before they are seen drop out of the count.
- More than most of a screen above the latest with nothing new, the pill shows as a compact round jump button. Turn it off with `Style(showsJumpButton: false)`.
- `alwaysFollow` jumps to the bottom for chosen items even when scrolled up. Pass `\.isMine` so the reader's own sent message always comes into view.
- `onReachTop` loads older items when the reader nears the top (within about three quarters of a screen). Prepend them to `items` before it returns: the rows on screen stay exactly where they are, including when the reader is resting on the very top edge. A small spinner floats at the top while it runs. It never runs twice at once, it runs again while a short list still reaches the top, and a call that adds nothing is not repeated until the reader scrolls away and back, so an exhausted history or a failed request never loops.
- Stays pinned to the bottom when the container changes size while following: the keyboard (for a composer in `safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`), rotation, or the last row growing as text streams into it. Scrolled up, the reader's place holds through all of these.
- Replacing the whole array (another conversation, a new filter) starts again at the latest with the count cleared. Removing the newest items is not treated as a reset.
- Only a scroll by the reader leaves the bottom: rows inserted above, lazy rows re-measuring and resizes never do. On iOS 18 and later a finger on the content also holds back any automatic scroll until the gesture ends.
- `isFollowing` (optional binding) reflects whether the view is pinned, for a "Live" badge or a toolbar button; set it to `true` to jump to the latest.
- `empty` is shown centered while there are no items. Rows are laid out in a `LazyVStack`, so long threads and logs stay cheap.
- Tracking: iOS 18 and later read `onScrollGeometryChange` and `onScrollPhaseChange`; iOS 17 measures the content in the scroll view's coordinate space. Both feed the same logic, and position is held through `scrollPosition(id:anchor:)` on the bottom-most row.
- Pill: Liquid Glass on iOS 26 (`glassEffect(.regular.interactive())`), a material with a soft shadow earlier, and a solid surface under Reduce Transparency.
- VoiceOver: the pill is a button read as its label ("3 new") with the hint "Jump to latest"; the scroll view offers a "Jump to latest" action while scrolled up; new items that arrive while scrolled up are announced once per burst with the final count.
- Localization: `newItemLabel` gives the pill text and the announcement. The default is a localized "3 new" through `String(localized:)`; add plural variants in your String Catalog, or pass your own. The pill mirrors in right-to-left layouts.
- Dynamic Type: the pill and its dot scale, the label wraps to two lines at the largest sizes, and the hit area never drops below 44pt.
- Reduce Motion: jumps are instant instead of scrolling, and the pill fades rather than scaling in.
- Haptics: a light tap when the pill first appears with news, and a soft one when you jump. Nothing fires for each new item.
- `Style` sets the pill's label, signal dot and ink, the Reduce Transparency surface, the spinner color, row spacing, horizontal and vertical padding, the follow threshold and whether the compact jump button shows.
