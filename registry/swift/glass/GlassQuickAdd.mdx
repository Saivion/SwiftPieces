## Notes

- One glass card that changes what it is. At rest it is a Quick add pill. Tap it and it grows up and across into a card listing the capture kinds, each keyed by a tinted disc; pick one and the same glass re-forms in place into that kind's capture: a header with the kind and a close button, a title field (up to three lines) and the kind's option chips. The card's 26pt continuous corner makes the 52pt pill a capsule and the open card a rounded panel, so the shape never changes kind, only size, on the morph spring. Each stage's contents blur out as the next blur in.
- It grows from its lower trailing corner, so place it at the bottom of its space (a screen's bottom trailing corner, above a tab bar) and it opens upward.
- Save is a signal bubble joined under the card's trailing corner. It buds out of the card the moment the field has text and melts back into the corner if the field empties, so it is only ever there when there is something to save. Saving (or Return) calls `onSave` with the kind, the trimmed text and the options that are on, then plays its own beat before anything closes: Save shows a spinner and morphs letter by letter to Saving, then turns sage with a check and Saved, with a success tap, then melts home and the card folds down into the pill. Each stage's contents leave quickly and the next arrive a beat later, so they never smear together as the glass resizes.
- The field takes focus as a capture opens, so the keyboard rises with it. Chips are plain content inside the card's glass: a soft fill, stronger when on; any number can be on.
- Pass `stage` to open the menu or a capture from your code (`.closed`, `.menu`, `.capture(id)`), `text` to keep a draft or fill it in, and change `saveTrigger` to save from your code (a keyboard shortcut, a toolbar button) as if Save were tapped. Without them the piece keeps its own.
- Haptics: a soft impact as the pill opens, a success tap as Saved lands.
- On iOS 26 the card and Save are Liquid Glass and Save's neck forms against the card; before that they are frosted glass, and under Reduce Transparency they are solid.
- Reduce Motion: the card resizes on a short spring with no overshoot, Save fades in and out where it rests instead of budding, and the stage swaps cross-fade.
- VoiceOver: the pill, every kind, each chip (with its selected state), Close and Save are buttons; saving is announced. Text follows Dynamic Type.
- `Style` (`GlassQuickAddStyle`) sets the label, secondary, ink, action (Save) and success (Saved) colours. `Kind` (`GlassQuickAddKind`) sets each kind's id, symbol, title, prompt, options and disc tint. All text and symbols are semibold.
