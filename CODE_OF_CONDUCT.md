# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a component library, not a place worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about taste in API design, not about people.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This breaks Dynamic Type at accessibility sizes" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.
- Accept that maintainers say no. A piece can be well built and still not belong in the library.
- Respect the scope. Pro is closed source on purpose, and asking repeatedly for it to be opened is not a contribution.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, commits or anywhere else connected to the project.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions, brigading a thread, or derailing a review.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions and the registry itself, and it applies when you are representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report anything that breaks the above to **conduct@swiftpieces.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and stay private. Include links and enough context to understand what happened.

Expect a response within a few days. Depending on severity, the outcome is a private warning, a temporary block from interaction, or a permanent ban from the repository. Maintainers who do not follow this document face the same consequences as anyone else.

If you are on the receiving end of an enforcement decision and think it was wrong, say so in a reply. It will be reconsidered once.
